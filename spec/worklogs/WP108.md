# WP108 — Quality prerequisites and catalog hygiene

The second Phase X foundation. `<required>` and `<forbidden>` blocks are parsed and enforced, so
the metatype- and Talent-gated qualities that WP114/WP115 offer have their gates in place first.
Nothing new is offered yet.

## What landed

- **`Requirement(mode, conditions)`** in `chummer5.data.qualities`, and `Quality.required` /
  `Quality.forbidden`. Each is a tuple of `<oneof>`/`<allof>` groups holding the verbatim
  `(kind, value)` pairs. `forbidden_names` stays as WP55's names-only view.
- **`GateContext` + `requirement_failure`** in `chummer5.engine.qualities`. It is one evaluator for
  both blocks. `metatype=` compares the metatype name. `quality=<talent>` compares `magic_type`
  (`TALENT_QUALITY_NAMES`). Any other `quality=` looks at held qualities, including the metatype's
  free ones. `magenabled` means any `magic_type`. Every other kind (`metatypecategory`,
  `critterpower`, `resenabled`, ...) is never met.
- **Enforcement at both ends.** `offerable_quality_catalog` hides a row whose metatype/Talent gate
  fails. `create_quality_selection` takes `metatype_name` and `granted_quality_names` and rejects a
  failing pick. Its WP55 mutual-exclusion check now runs through the same evaluator, keeping its
  exact wording.
- **`OUT_OF_SCOPE_QUALITY_NAMES`**: the five talent qualities, Astral Perception, and the two
  Infected powers are excluded by name in `mvp_quality_catalog`. A test pins the catalog shape: 96
  SR5 rows, 8 out of scope, 24 offered today, and 88 if every in-scope row were offerable.
- **The rulebook-wins override**: `_RULEBOOK_REQUIRED_ADDITIONS` adds Adept to Spirit Affinity's
  gate (p. 77). It was the only disagreement. The full comparison is in `learnings/phase-x.md`.
- **Stale picks (WP19 path).** `_quality_selection_context` re-validates the committed picks against
  the current character and sets `quality_budget_warning`. The Qualities page shows it, the nav
  marks the step, and it feeds the priorities form's aggregate stale flag.

## Decisions

### Talent names are `magic_type`, not qualities

The legacy engine grants a Magician a *quality* named "Magician" and gates on it. This engine keeps
the Talent in `Character.magic_type`, whose values are the same strings. So `quality=Magician`
resolves against `magic_type` and no fake quality is invented. Magic Resistance's p. 76 rule then
falls out of its own `forbidden` list.

### Offered-row filtering ignores quality-vs-quality conflicts

`offerable_quality_catalog` evaluates gates with only the metatype's free qualities as "held", not
the player's picks. Both SINner variants should stay visible so the player can pick one of them.
Taking both is `create_quality_selection`'s rejection to make. Only what the player can't fix on
this page, their metatype and Talent, hides a row.

### A missing metatype fails closed

`create_quality_selection`'s new `metatype_name` defaults to `None`, and then every `metatype=`
condition is unmet. A caller that forgets to pass it gets a rejection of a metatype-gated pick. It
does not get a silent pass. Every production caller (the route, `build_character`, the synthetic
harness) passes it.

### Error wording lists only what a player could act on

A failed `required` group names only core metatypes, the three playable talents, and "a Magic
rating". Astral Beacon's gate lists fifteen names, mostly critters and Infected. The message reads
"Astral Beacon requires Adept or Aspected Magician or Magician or a Magic rating".

## Traps hit

- The plan's acceptance ("an Elf is offered Human-Looking", "a Magician is not offered Magic
  Resistance") names qualities that only WP114 makes offerable. The tests force the real catalog row
  offerable with `dataclasses.replace(..., bonus_support=NO_EFFECT)`, so the real gate data is what's
  exercised.
- The first real stale case turned out to exist already: a Human who buys Low-Light Vision and then
  becomes an Elf, who gets it free. Before this WP that pick silently stayed committed. It now shows
  "not an MVP-offered quality id" as a warning, and that is the web test.
- The phase intro's "four DocWagon lifestyle qualities" are not in `qualities.xml` at all. They live
  in `lifestyles.xml`, so they belong to WP132, not to this exclusion list.
