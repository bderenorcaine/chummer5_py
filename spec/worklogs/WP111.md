# WP111 — Initiative variants: Astral and Matrix

This WP covers the remaining Step Eight initiative rows. Living Persona is out of scope
(Technomancer).

## What landed

- **`astral_initiative(character)`**: `INT × 2`, or `None` for anyone but a Magician.
  `ASTRAL_INITIATIVE_DICE = 2`.
- **Matrix AR**: no new function. The sheet shows the physical Initiative again under its own
  label, because p. 229 says AR *is* the physical Initiative, augmentations included.
- **`matrix_vr_initiative(character)`** returns a `MatrixVRInitiative` (Data Processing, the device
  it came from, whether it's assumed from a deck's array, and whether hot-sim is available), or
  `None` when the character can't enter VR. `COLD_SIM_INITIATIVE_DICE = 3` and
  `HOT_SIM_INITIATIVE_DICE = 4`.
- **Character sheet**: Astral (Magicians only), Matrix AR (everyone), and Matrix VR cold/hot (only
  when available). Each VR row carries a note naming the device its Data Processing came from.

## Research, and what it decided

The rulebook research is in `learnings/phase-x.md` and summarised here:

- **Perceiving vs projecting.** The phase file asked whether an astrally perceiving character needs
  Astral Initiative. It doesn't. p. 159 has the magician swap Initiatives on the action that
  *projects*, and p. 313 limits projection to "full Magicians". An Adept with Astral Perception, or
  an Aspected Magician, perceives from their body on the physical Initiative.
- **2D6 or 3D6.** There is a real printed conflict. The decision is logged in `decisions-log.md` as
  open for a user ruling, and the value is one constant.
- **Which devices give VR.** VR needs three things: a sim module, a DNI, and a persona device.
  Every source of each comes from pp. 227, 438, 439, 451 and 452. Trodes, an implanted datajack,
  commlink, cyberdeck or control rig are a DNI. The Sim Module upgrade, an implanted commlink or a
  control rig give cold-sim. Any cyberdeck or the hot Sim Module upgrade gives hot-sim. The name
  sets are private constants in `chummer5.engine.derived`.
- **Which Data Processing.** It is the persona device's. A cyberdeck wins over a commlink, since
  that's what a decker runs. A deck's DP is an unrecorded array assignment, so the sheet assumes
  the highest value and says so in a note.

## Decisions

### A plain commlink gives AR only

The acceptance criterion left the answer open ("shows whatever the research concludes"). A
commlink has no sim module unless the upgrade is bought, and a sim module is useless without a DNI.
The mundane commlink-only character therefore gets exactly one extra row, Matrix AR. The web test
buys Trodes and a Sim Module and watches the cold-sim row appear.

### VR dice ignore physical augmentations

Legacy's cold/hot dice are `3/4 + MatrixInitiativeDice improvements`. Wired reflexes add
`InitiativeDice`, not Matrix dice, so they don't help in VR. That matches p. 229's "+3D6". No
Matrix-dice source exists in this project's scope, so the constants are the whole formula.
