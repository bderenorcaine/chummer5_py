# WP113 — Racial traits: Lifestyle multiplier, Troll Reach and dermal armor

This WP completes the Metatype Attribute Table's racial column.

## What landed

- **`Metatype.lifestyle_cost_percent` / `reach` / `armor` / `armor_group`**, parsed from the three
  `<bonus>` children the SR5 base metatypes carry. There is also `Metatype.unmodeled_effect_note`,
  which only the Troll has, for its Reach.
- **`ImprovementType.LIFESTYLE_COST` (percent) and `REACH`**, plus
  **`apply_metatype_bonus_improvements(character)`**, which adds them and the racial `ARMOR` (in its
  `group0`) as `METATYPE`-sourced improvements keyed by the metatype id.
  `build_character`, the synthetic harness and the web's `_relink_metatype_qualities` all call it.
- **`LifestyleSelection.cost_percent` / `monthly_cost`**. `create_lifestyle_selection(...,
  cost_percent=)` prices `surcharged_monthly_cost(base, percent) × months`, rounded up to the nuyen.
  `lifestyle_cost_percent(character)` is the Σ. This is also the hook WP115's Dependents and WP132's
  lifestyle options will use.
- **Relinking re-prices.** `_relink_metatype_qualities` now drops *every* `METATYPE`-sourced
  Improvement, because the racial ones are keyed by the previous metatype's id, which it can't know.
  It re-applies both kinds and re-prices a committed Lifestyle at the new percent, so a Human who
  becomes a Troll sees their Low Lifestyle double at once.
- **Character sheet**: a Reach row (only when nonzero, using the legacy `String_Reach` key). The
  unmodeled-effects collector reads `character.metatype` too, so the Troll's note shows.
- **Registry**: a new "Troll +1 Reach" row, and the Dwarf pathogen/toxin row reworded to cite the
  metatype (+2 dice, p. 66) instead of the purchasable quality's +1/8-Karma framing.

## Decisions

### +100%, not +50%

The chapter's Rob paragraph says "+50% gear/Lifestyle costs", with its own caveat that the troll
figures "are for row-B/no-Karma illustration only". The Metatype Attribute Table, p. 420's troll tax
("their Lifestyle costs are doubled"), the data, and Rob's own Step Six arithmetic (12,000¥ for 3
months Low) all say +100%.

### The rigger fixture had to change

Halla "Belfry" Ødegård is a troll, and her Medium × 9 months was priced without the troll tax. With
it applied, her vehicles no longer fit the budget. The fix keeps her ¥4,255 remainder and every
other number. She now leases the garage for 4 months (¥40,000 at the troll rate) and buys a second,
Rating-2 Fake SIN for the van's paperwork (+¥5,000). Her `unmodeled_notes:names` gains "Troll". The
fixture comment records the old figures. The save-path test now reads the fixture's lifestyle
instead of a hard-coded copy.

### Racial dermal armor is `group0`, like the data says

The WP109 `group` → `unique_name` mapping means Dermal Plating 2 on a troll gives Armor 2 from
augmentations, not 3. WP129 decides whether that is *replace* or *highest wins* in the rulebook's
eyes. For the values in play the two readings agree.
