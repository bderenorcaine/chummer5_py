# WP114 — Positive qualities: the remaining core catalog

This WP offers every remaining in-scope core positive quality. Each quality's computed part is
modeled, and the rest is registered. Aptitude is WP116's. Mentor Spirit was already widened per
character in WP48.

## What landed

- **Computed (4)**: Toughness (`DAMAGE_RESISTANCE`), High Pain Tolerance 1-3
  (`CM_THRESHOLD_OFFSET` × rating), Will to Live 1-3 (`CM_OVERFLOW` × rating), and Photographic
  Memory (the new `MEMORY` type). The classifier learnt `<damageresistance>`, `<memory>` and a
  one-leaf `<conditionmonitor>` (`thresholdoffset` / `overflow`).
- **The Memory derived stat**: `derived.memory(character)` = LOG + WIL + Σ MEMORY (p. 152). It
  appears on the sheet, and WP128's Mnemonic Enhancer will feed it too.
- **Choice + registry (3)**: Codeslinger (`choice_kind="matrix_action"`, 31 actions from the new
  `chummer5.data.actions`), Home Ground (`"home_ground"`, 5 bonus ids translated as
  `Web_HomeGround_*`) and Spirit Affinity (`"spirit_type"`, the 6 core types from
  `load_core_spirit_types`). `choice_options(quality, magic_type)` is the single option source for
  the validator and the dropdown. `_quality_choice_label.html` renders a choice by kind on both the
  form and the sheet.
- **Registry only (12)**: Ambidextrous, Analytical Mind, Astral Chameleon, Blandness, First
  Impression, Focused Concentration, Human-Looking, Magic Resistance, Quick Healer, and the three
  purchasable Resistance rows. These are name-gated `NO_EFFECT` with notes. Blandness and First
  Impression still apply their Notoriety through WP110's separate field.
- **The Dwarf's racial row**: `METATYPE_ONLY_QUALITY_NAMES` keeps "Resistance to Pathogens/Toxins"
  unpurchasable. `_RULEBOOK_FORBIDDEN_ADDITIONS` makes the three purchasable rows forbidden
  alongside it, so a Dwarf is never offered them.
- **15 registry rows** have been added to the Project Overview, generated from the same note text
  so the two mirrors match.
- **Offered count**: 26 → 45. The "every purchasable in-scope row" target is now 87, down from 88,
  because the racial row is metatype-only.

## Decisions

- **The gates already did the per-character work.** Human-Looking, Magic Resistance, Focused
  Concentration, Astral Chameleon and Spirit Affinity became offerable, and WP108's gates hide them
  from the characters who can't take them. Three tests that compared a mundane Human's offer list
  to the whole catalog now subtract the gated rows explicitly.
- **Spirit Affinity offers all six core types**, not just the tradition's five. An Adept has no
  tradition but is a "magic user" (WP108's ruling), and the quality is a registry row anyway.
- **Wesson now imports Ambidextrous.** His positive Karma goes from 10 to 14 and Karma remaining
  from 9 to 5, and "Ambidextrous" leaves his dropped list. It is the intended effect: an offered
  quality imports.
