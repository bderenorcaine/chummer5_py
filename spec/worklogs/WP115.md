# WP115 — Negative qualities: the remaining core catalog

This WP offers every remaining in-scope core negative quality except the four that change purchase
rules (Incompetent, Uncouth, Uneducated, Sensitive System), which are WP117's. Each quality's
computed part is modeled, and the rest is registered.

## What landed

- **Computed (5 rows)**: Dependents ×3 (`LIFESTYLE_COST` +10/20/30%, from the data's
  `<lifestylecost>`), Low Pain Tolerance (`CM_THRESHOLD` −1, so a wound modifier every 2 boxes), and
  Loss of Confidence (−2 dice on the chosen skill, and its specialization switched off). The
  classifier learnt `<lifestylecost>`, `<conditionmonitor><threshold>`, `<skillcategory>` and the
  `<selectskill><val>` shape.
- **Three new Improvement types**, because these are dice-pool modifiers and not rating changes:
  `SKILL_POOL` (by skill name), `SKILL_CATEGORY_POOL` (by category) and `SPECIALIZATION_DISABLED`.
  `skills.dice_pool` reads all three, including when the skill is defaulted. `SKILL` still means
  rating, so Loss of Confidence doesn't lower the rating its own prerequisite checks.
- **Addiction ×4, Allergy ×8, Prejudiced ×6** are `SELECT_TEXT` and repeatable. `SELECT_TEXT`
  qualities now parse their other `<bonus>` children too, which is how Burnout keeps its −3 Social
  Active. Severe's −2 is a rulebook-wins addition (`_RULEBOOK_BONUS_ADDITIONS`).
- **Choices**: Scorched (`scorched_effect`, the 5 rows of p. 83's table, translated as
  `Web_Scorched_*`), Spirit Bane (`spirit_type`, reusing WP114's list) and Loss of Confidence
  (`skill`, every non-Resonance active skill, labelled through `dterm("skills.xml")`).
- **Registry only (11)**: Astral Beacon, Bad Luck, Bad Rep, Combat Paralysis, Distinctive Style,
  Elf Poser, Gremlins 1-4, Insomnia (Basic/Full), Ork Poser, Weak Immune System. Their gates and
  mutual exclusions were already parsed (WP55/WP108) and now fire.
- **`LaterStepFacts`**: the skill ratings and cyberdeck ownership that Loss of Confidence's rating
  4+ and Scorched's "BTL Addiction or decker" prerequisites need. See the decisions log for why
  a save or import skips them.
- **Dependents re-price a committed Lifestyle.** `_replace_committed_qualities` now calls the same
  re-pricing helper WP113's metatype relink used (factored out as `_reprice_lifestyle`).
- **Legacy import reads choices from `<extra>`** for every choice-bearing quality. It tries the
  text verbatim, as "Spirit of X", and squashed to PascalCase.
- **Offered count**: 45 → 81. The only unoffered rows left are Aptitude (WP116), the four WP117
  qualities, Mentor Spirit (widened per character), the Dwarf's racial row and the 8 out-of-scope
  rows. A test pins that list.
- **28 registry rows** have been added to the Project Overview, one per row or per severity family,
  generated from the note text.

## Decisions

- **Loss of Confidence needs its skill bought first.** The pick is checked at pick time against the
  character's current skill ratings, as the plan asks. A player who picks qualities before skills
  gets "needs a skill at rating 4 or higher; Pistols is at 0". That is accurate, and the book's own
  wording ("one that the character [...] has invested in building") supports it.
- **The Talent-change path leaves later-step facts out.** `_strip_mentor_spirit_selection`
  re-validates the remaining picks. With the facts included, a Loss of Confidence that a skill edit
  had made stale would raise there instead of staying a warning.
- **Weak Immune System is closed to Dwarfs, and Addiction (Severe) gets −2 Social.** Both are
  rulebook-wins calls. They are recorded in the decisions log with the Scorched detection rules.
- **Registry rows are grouped by family** in the Project Overview (Allergy by severity, Prejudiced
  by degree). Each catalog row still carries its own note.

## Traps

- **An `ElementTree` element with no children is falsy.** `elem.find(...) or default` silently takes
  the default. The `minimumrating` read uses an explicit `is None` check instead.
- **`FlatBonus` is defined below the forbidden-additions table** in `data/qualities.py`. The new
  `_RULEBOOK_BONUS_ADDITIONS` table has to sit after the class.
- **No fixture assertion moved.** Serpent now imports its Prejudiced (Specific, Biased), but no test
  pins Serpent's Karma. The fixtures that hold other new negatives (Bastion, Glessner, Spirit_Warden,
  ...) are all blocked earlier, for other reasons.
