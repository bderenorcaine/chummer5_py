# WP119 — Adept powers with computed effects, and power instances

Power picks are now instances with a target, and the powers whose effect this engine can compute
are offered, along with three target-only registry powers.

## What landed

- **Data** (`data/powers.py`): `Power` gained `max_levels`, `extra_point_cost`, the target fields
  (`target_kind` attribute/skill/text, `target_options`, `target_skill_categories`,
  `target_excluded`, `target_minimum_rating`), `forbidden_qualities`, and a `repeatable` property.
  `PowerFlatBonus` gained `unique_name`. The classifier learnt `<initiativepass>`, `<armor>`, a
  `<specificattribute>` with `val`, `<conditionmonitor><thresholdoffset>` and Improved Ability's
  `<selectskill><val>Rating`. It skips `<disablequality>` (Lightning Reflexes, not core). A new
  `NO_EFFECT` value covers Critical Strike, Enhanced Accuracy and Killing Hands.
- **Engine** (`engine/magic.py`):
  - `PowerPick(power, rating, target)`, with `power_points` = `points × rating + extra`.
  - `power_target_options`, and `power_max_rating` (MAG, `<maxlevels>`, and Improved Ability's
    ⌈learned / 2⌉).
  - `learned_skill_ratings(character, skills)`: the bought rating plus the Talent grant.
  - `create_power_selection` checks instance counts, distinct targets, target validity, the
    optional learned ratings, and forbidden qualities.
  - `apply_power_improvements` targets `flat_bonus.target_name or pick.target` and carries
    `unique_name`.
- **Offered now**: Improved Reflexes 1-3 (REA + Initiative dice, `precedence0`), Improved Ability
  (skill rating), Mystic Armor (Armor), Pain Resistance (wound offset), plus the three registry
  powers. Improved Physical Attribute can be bought for several attributes. Enhanced Perception now
  levels up to MAG.
- **Web**: the powers form has per-instance rows (`power_rating_{id}` / `{id}:n`) and a target
  column (a select, or text for a text target). It warns when a committed pick stops fitting. The
  sheet shows a target by kind (`_power_target_label.html`).
- **Save/import**: the save's `target` key, with `selected_attribute` still read. Legacy import maps
  `<extra>` onto any target kind: Wesson now imports Enhanced Accuracy (Pistols) and Improved
  Ability (Pistols) 1.
- **High Pain Tolerance ⟷ Pain Resistance** is enforced from both sides.
- **Registry**: five Project Overview rows (Critical Strike, Enhanced Accuracy, Killing Hands, Mystic
  Armor, Pain Resistance).

## Decisions

See the decisions log (2026-09-26, WP119): the `<limit>` correction, the key rename, Improved
Ability's facts, Improved Reflexes' Reaction, and the High Pain Tolerance exclusion.

## Traps

- **`learned_skill_ratings` first returned `{}` when no skill spend was committed**, which dropped the
  Adept's free Talent skill. An Adept who picks Improved Ability right after the Talent step was
  rejected. The web test caught it.
- **A `sed` over the tests renamed an unrelated `match="not eligible"`** (the free-skill grant tests)
  as well as the power one. Those three were reverted by hand.
