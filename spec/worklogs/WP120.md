# WP120 — The remaining adept powers

All 27 in-scope core powers are now offerable. Improved Potential (Chaos Mentor) is excluded by
name.

## What landed

- **13 name-gated `NO_EFFECT` powers** (`data/powers._VERIFIED_NO_EFFECT_NAMES`), each with a note:
  Adrenaline Boost, Attribute Boost, Combat Sense, Danger Sense, Improved Sense, Kinesics, Light
  Body, Missile Parry, Natural Immunity, Rapid Healing, Spell Resistance, Traceless Walk and Wall
  Running. WP119's target plumbing supplies the two that name something:
  - Attribute Boost: one of AGI/BOD/REA/STR, up to 4 instances.
  - Improved Sense: free text, one instance per distinct sense.
- **`engine/magic.OUT_OF_SCOPE_POWER_NAMES`** holds the Chaos Mentor row. `mvp_power_catalog`
  excludes it even though it is `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` anyway, the same belt-and-braces approach
  `OUT_OF_SCOPE_QUALITY_NAMES` takes.
- **13 Project Overview rows.**
- **Attribute Boost** keeps the 2026-07-19 caution. Its `flat_bonuses` stays empty, a test applies
  it and checks AGI is unchanged, and its note says the sheet's attribute isn't raised.

## Consequences

- **Wesson now imports Attribute Boost (AGI) and Combat Sense 4.** With WP119's two, he holds four of
  his six legacy powers. Motion Sense and Rapid Draw are SG-sourced and stay dropped. That is 3.0 of
  his 4 Power Points; the missing 1.0 is the two SG powers.
- **Fixture-shaped tests moved from "unsupported" to "registry"**: the Attribute Boost regression
  test, the empty-`<bonus>` powers test, the Danger Sense field check, and the Wesson power table.
  The "reject an unsupported power" test now uses Chaos Mentor.
