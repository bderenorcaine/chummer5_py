# WP121 — Aspected Magician

The Aspected Magician is now an offered Talent option at B, C and D (Magic 5/3/2).

## What landed

- **Data**: `TalentOption` gained `skill_group_choices` / `skill_group_qty` / `skill_group_val`
  (`<skillgroupchoices>` / `<skillgroupqty>` / `<skillgroupval>`).
- **Engine** (`engine/magic.py`):
  - `OFFERED_TALENT_CODENAMES` gains "Aspected Magician".
  - `create_magic_selection(aspect=)` requires a tradition and an aspect from the option's choices,
    rejects picked skills, and derives the grant as the aspect group's skills at `skill_group_val`
    (none at D).
  - `MagicSelection.aspect` and `Character.magic_aspect` carry it.
  - `uses_tradition` / `casts_spells` / `binds_spirits` are the capability checks.
- **Rules**: `skill_purchase_rules` bans the other two groups (and so Counterspelling for a
  non-Sorcery aspect). `create_bound_spirit_selection` rejects a non-Conjuring aspect. Drain uses the
  tradition, and a mentor gives its Magician slot. Mentor Spirit's quality check accepts the
  Aspected Magician.
- **Web**:
  - The Talent fragment shows the tradition picker, an aspect picker, and the grant line.
  - The spell and spirit sections gate on `spells_available` / `spirits_available` flags from their
    contexts. The step shell's tab availability and resource panel use the capability helpers.
  - The sheet prints "Aspect: …".
  - The powers section's WP119 warning now also marks the nav, a missed key from WP119.
- **Save**: `magic_aspect`, with `granted_skill_ids` left empty for an aspected save. **Legacy
  import**: an explicit blocking reason.
- **Talent-change matrix**: switching Magician → Aspected resets spells, powers, spirits and foci
  (WP29's rule). A bought skill of a now-banned group stays, with the skills page warning. Tested.

## Decisions

See the decisions log (2026-09-26, WP121) and the learnings entry: foci open to every aspect,
Enchanting's work beyond its skills unmodeled, the grant derived from the aspect, legacy import
blocked, and the Incompetent edge case left to WP135.

## Verification

- `uv run python -m pytest -q`: 1619 passed. `tests/unit/test_aspected_magician_wp121.py` covers:
  - the three letters' ratings and grants, and the validation errors;
  - capabilities per aspect and the group bans (with the ungrouped skills still open);
  - Drain, the mentor slot, and no Astral Initiative;
  - the plan's acceptance through the page: a B Conjurer has Banishing/Binding/Summoning 0 (4),
    binds a spirit, and has no spell list;
  - a Sorcerer's Karma-only spell list and no spirits;
  - a save round trip of the aspect, the Magician → Aspected warning, and the legacy block.
- `ruff check`, `ruff format --check`, `mypy src`: clean.
