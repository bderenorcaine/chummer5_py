# WP122 — Mentor spirit bonuses, complete

Before this WP, 17 of the 51 core mentor slots were applied, and an Adept following Bear or Eagle
got nothing. Now every slot is applied except Rat's reagent harvesting, which has no `<bonus>` and
is verified inert.

## What landed

- **Data** (`data/mentors.py`): `_parse_slot` recognizes every core shape.
  - `FlatBonus` gains `damage_resistance` and an empty-target "skill of choice".
  - New `ConditionalPool` (a spell category's Spellcasting/Ritual Spellcasting/Alchemy, or
    Summoning for one spirit type) and `FreePower` (with the `<bonusoverride>` narrowing).
  - `MentorChoice` gains `conditional_pools`, `free_powers`, `skill_choice_categories` and
    `granted_qualities`.
  - `MentorSpiritArchetype` gains an `unmodeled_effect_note`: the disadvantage, condensed, for 15
    archetypes. Eagle's is its granted Allergy.
- **Engine**:
  - `MentorSpiritPick` gains `skill_choice`, `free_powers` (`PowerPick`s), `granted_qualities` and
    `chosen_power_targets`.
  - `create_quality_selection(mentor_spirit_skill_choice=, mentor_spirit_power_targets=)` validates
    them. New helpers: `mentor_skill_choice_options`, `mentor_free_power_options`,
    `mentor_free_powers_needing_a_target`.
  - `apply_mentor_spirit_improvements` applies the skill of choice and the free powers.
  - `magic.power_pick_improvements` is the shared per-instance applier.
- **Web**: the qualities page asks for the skill of choice and each free power's target. The sheet
  lists the mentor's skill of choice, free powers, granted Allergy, and conditional pools. The notes
  collector walks the free powers and granted qualities too.
- **Save**: `mentor_spirit_skill_choice` and `mentor_spirit_power_targets`.
- **Registry**: 15 Project Overview rows, one per archetype disadvantage.
- The data module's "applies nothing at all" paragraph is gone. Its classification section is
  rewritten.

## Decisions

See the decisions log (2026-09-26, WP122): conditional pools are shown rather than applied, free
powers live on the pick, and Eagle's Allergy is granted. One known gap: a free level plus a bought
level of the same power isn't capped jointly.

## Traps

- **Dog's Adept slot broke three existing tests.** Its two free Improved Sense powers now need
  targets, and the tests built a Dog Adept without any. They now pass targets, which is the
  intended new rule.
- **A 123-character comment line had slipped into WP121's commit.** Ruff caught it here and it's
  fixed in this commit.

## Verification

- `uv run python -m pytest -q`: 1627 passed. `tests/unit/test_mentor_bonuses_wp122.py` covers:
  - the plan's acceptance: an Adept following Bear gets Damage Resistance +2 and Rapid Healing 1,
    and one following Eagle gets Perception +2, Combat Sense 1 and the 0-Karma Allergy;
  - Dragonslayer's skill of choice;
  - free-power targets: Sea's athletic narrowing, Thunderbird's count, Wolf's auto-filled Agility;
  - no free powers for a Magician;
  - a Bear Magician's "Spellcasting: 10 dice for Health spells..." and the registry note on the
    sheet.

  The mentors tests were rewritten from "unsupported" to what each shape now does.
- `ruff check`, `ruff format --check`, `mypy src`: clean.
