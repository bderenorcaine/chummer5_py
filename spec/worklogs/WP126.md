# WP126 — Availability and Device Rating caps for scalable items

This WP applies Step Six's "Availability 12 or less, Device Rating 6 or less" (p. 94, repeated on
p. 98) as the ruling scoped it: it caps items whose rating drives their Availability or Device
Rating, and leaves flat over-cap items offered.

## Research

See the learnings entry. There are two page cites for the rule, the Device Ratings table (p. 421),
and the sensors' printed "Rating 2-8" (p. 446). Wired Reflexes' per-rating Availability is on
p. 456.

The survey of all SR5 rated cyberware `<avail>` values found these shapes:

- **Scaled** (`Rating * N`, optionally parenthesized, R/F suffix): 17 rows.
- **One per-rating list**: Wired Reflexes, `FixedValues(8R,12R,20R)`.
- **Fixed on a rated row**: Retinal Duplication `16F`, Ultrasound Sensor `10`, Hydraulic Jacks `9`,
  Essence Hole/Antihole `0`.
- **One parent-relative form**: Customized Agility/Strength, `+Rating - MinRating + 1`.

## What landed

- **Data**:
  - `chummer5.data.gear.availability_at_rating(avail, rating)` covers WP66's forms plus
    `FixedValues`.
  - `CyberwareEntry.raw_availability` holds the verbatim `<avail>`.
- **Engine** (`engine/cyberware_subsystems.py`):
  - `availability_capped_maximum(item, min, max)` returns the highest rating within 12 for a row
    whose Availability actually varies with its rating. Any other row keeps its full range, and a
    scaled row is never capped below its minimum.
  - `cyberware_rating_bounds(cap_availability=)` offers that capped range.
  - `create_cyberware_tree_selection(availability_exempt=)` rejects a rating over the cap unless
    its `(item id, rating)` is in the exempt set.
  - `purchased_ratings(selection)` builds that set from a committed tree.
- **Engine** (`engine/resources.py`): `MAX_DEVICE_RATING`, `DEVICE_RATING_GEAR_CATEGORIES`.
  `build_starter_gear_catalog` clamps those categories' rated items to 6.
- **Web** (`routes/cyberware.py`):
  - The rating dropdowns offer the capped range, widened to the unit's current rating.
  - The submit passes the committed tree as the exempt set.
- **Docs**:
  - The Project Overview's standing Availability backfill note is retired.
  - `cyberware_formula`'s and `cyberware_subsystems`' "ruled out project-wide" lines are corrected.
  - A decisions-log entry.

## Decisions

- **Grandfathering instead of a hard cap.** See the decisions log. The engine check is opt-in, and
  the web route opts in with "what you already had". The result:
  - a new over-cap pick is rejected;
  - an old one survives edits elsewhere;
  - saves and legacy files load unchanged.
- **"Scales with rating" means the Availability differs between the lowest and highest rating.**
  That is the literal ruling ("cap only the items whose rating scales their Availability"). It
  keeps Retinal Duplication (16F at every rating) offered, as the ruling wants.
- **The gear cap stays catalog-level**, like WP66's. Only sensors 7-8 change. The load risk is in
  the decisions log.
- **Rated bioware isn't offered yet.** WP128 offers it, and can reuse `availability_capped_maximum`
  (its entries carry the same `<avail>` shapes). Grades (WP127) will shift Availability before the
  cap reads it.

## Traps

- An existing test bought a rating-6 Select Sound Filter (Availability 18) to trigger a Capacity
  error. It now fails on Availability first, so it now overflows Capacity with two in-cap plugins.

## Verification

- `uv run python -m pytest -q`: all pass (1662). `tests/unit/test_availability_caps_wp126.py`
  covers:
  - every `<avail>` form;
  - the capped ranges for six representative rows, including the plan's Muscle Replacement 1-2 and
    the uncapped Retinal Duplication;
  - reject-new versus keep-owned in the engine;
  - Single Sensor and Sensor Array at 6, against Arrow: Standard's untouched 12;
  - a web flow: the picker offers 1-2, a new 3 is rejected, and a loaded save with Muscle
    Replacement 3 survives an unrelated cyberware edit with its dropdown still offering 3.
- `ruff check`, `ruff format --check`, `mypy src`: clean.
