# WP128 — Rating-scaled bioware

This WP offers the 15 rated core bioware rows (pp. 459-461), each with a per-unit rating. The
computed effects are applied through the existing Improvement types plus one new one. The rest is
registered.

## Research

See the learnings entry for each row's printed effect. The one finding that changes the plan: Muscle
Toner and Muscle Augmentation are **not** incompatible with each other. Details are in the decisions
log.

## What landed

- **Data**:
  - **`data/bioware.py`**:
    - `load_bioware` adds `_parse_rated_bioware`, which takes the `Rating * N` cost/Essence
      formulas and the raw `<avail>`, and classifies the bonus with cyberware's `classify_bonus`.
    - `Bioware` gains `max_rating`, `cost_formula`, `essence_formula`, `raw_availability`,
      `rated_bonuses` and `incompatible_names`, plus `cost_at` / `essence_at` / `availability_at`.
      For a rated row, `cost`/`essence_cost`/`availability` hold its rating-1 values.
    - Seven registry notes, and the `REGISTERED_UNMODELED_BONUS_TAGS` they cover.
    - The catalog goes from 6 to 21 rows.
  - **`data/cyberware.py`**:
    - `classify_bonus(registered_tags=)`.
    - New shapes: the Mental/Social limit and Memory leaves, `<conditionmonitor>
      <sharedthresholdoffset>`, and `<skillgroup>` / `<skillcategory>` dice pools.
    - A conditioned `<skillgroup>` can be registered via `CONDITIONAL_SKILL_GROUP`.
    - No SR5 cyberware row uses any of these, so cyberware classification is unchanged. That was
      checked by enumerating the tags.
- **Engine**:
  - `ImprovementType.SKILL_GROUP_POOL`. `dice_pool` adds it. The knowledge and language pools add
    `SKILL_CATEGORY_POOL` for their category and for "Language".
  - `engine/cyberware.py`'s kind→type map has the five new kinds. The test that pins the map now
    includes bioware.
  - **`engine/bioware.py`**:
    - Requests and purchases carry a `rating`.
    - `nuyen_cost` / `essence_cost` are computed at that rating.
    - `bioware_rating_maximum` gives the Availability-12 cap at a grade.
    - A rated row may be bought once.
    - `other_augmentations` (built by `augmentation_names`) drives the incompatibility check.
    - Rated bonuses are applied through `cyberware_bonus_improvements`, re-sourced as BIOWARE.
- **Save**: a `rating` key on each bioware purchase. It is additive, with a default of 1.
- **Legacy import**:
  - A rated row imports at its stored `<rating>`, so Barrett's Mnemonic Enhancer now resolves.
  - The catalog-miss reason no longer says "rating-scaled".
- **Web**:
  - A per-unit rating select for a rated row, capped by Availability at the unit's grade and
    widened to the rating it already has.
  - A "{value} per rating" price column, and rating-aware subtotals and Essence.
  - The sheet shows "Muscle Toner (Rating 2)".
- **Project Overview**: seven "Unmodeled real effects" rows.

## Decisions

See the decisions log: the plan's Toner/Augmentation acceptance is overruled, cross-catalog
incompatibilities are enforced, a rated row is bought once, and Pain Editor / Suprathyroid stay out.

## Traps

- **`replace()` shares the Improvement index.** The new tests' first draft compared a character
  with its own augmented copy, and every delta came out 0. The helper now deep-copies the index. Any
  "before vs. after an Improvement" test needs the same care.
- **Seven existing tests assumed a 6-row, rated-free bioware catalog.** Those were the counts, the
  exclusion test, the empty registry, "the first loaded row has no note", the kind-map pin, the
  Barrett import reason, and the Mnemonic Enhancer rejection. Each was updated to the new rule
  rather than weakened. The rejection example is now Pain Editor (18F), with a positive rated-import
  test beside it.
