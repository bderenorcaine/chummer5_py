# WP130 — Weapon accessories

A purchased weapon can now carry the core firearm accessories: silencers, scopes, smartgun systems,
holsters, and the rest. Each one sits on a mount, one accessory per mount. What a weapon ships with
is included at no cost.

## Research

See the learnings entry: the p. 432 table, p. 431's one-accessory-per-mount rule, the printed "who
fits" text, and where each built-in sits according to the weapon descriptions. The data has 30 SR5
rows. 4 are hidden built-ins, so 26 are offered. Legacy's mount accounting is
`Weapon.GetAccessoryMountsAsync`: built-ins get the "Internal" mount unless the weapon's entry gives
them one, and no SR5 weapon does.

## What landed

- **Data** (`data/weapon_accessories.py`):
  - `load_weapon_accessories()` reads every `<accessory>` row. Only SR5, non-hidden rows are
    offerable, but built-ins still need the others resolved.
  - `load_weapon_mount_info()` gives each SR5 weapon's mounts, built-ins, Availability,
    `<allowaccessory>`, and the fields a requirement tests.
  - A small `Condition` parser for `<required>`/`<forbidden>`: AND leaves, `<OR>` groups,
    `equals`/`contains`/`lessthanequals`, and `<oneof><accessory>`. Anything unknown fails closed.
  - Printed overrides: the Smart Firing Platform's 2,500¥, and the three holsters' weapon
    categories.
  - A registry note for each offered accessory.
- **Engine** (`engine/weapon_accessories.py`):
  - `AccessoryPick(weapon, copy, accessory, mount)` is the request. `InstalledAccessory` is the
    validated, priced result.
  - `builtin_accessories()` and `free_mounts()`.
  - `accessory_problem()` checks every rule except the mount: core weapon, accepts accessories, no
    duplicate (except clips and speed loaders), requirement, prohibition, one ammunition-feed
    replacement, a matching mount, and Availability ≤ 12. For the internal smartgun, Availability is
    the weapon's + 2.
  - `create_weapon_accessories()` applies picks in order.
- **Resources**:
  - `create_resources_selection(weapon_accessories=)` counts the weapon copies in the flat gear,
    installs the picks, and adds their cost to `nuyen_spent`.
  - `ResourcesSelection.weapon_accessories` and `.accessory_picks`.
  - Every carry-forward call site passes the picks through: the vehicles, lifestyle, cyberware and
    foci routes, the shell, the sheet's display re-budget, and `build_character`'s three calls.
- **Bug fixed on the way**: the foci route re-validated resources with every gear item as a bare
  name. That priced Rating gear at 0 once foci were edited. It now uses `resources_gear_args`, like
  the other routes.
- **Derived**: the unmodeled-notes walk visits the accessories.
- **Save**: `weapon_accessories` (`{weapon, copy, accessory, mount}`), additive.
- **Web**:
  - The Resources panel has a "Weapon accessories" block, with one fieldset per purchased weapon
    copy. It shows built-ins with their mount, installed accessories as checked boxes (untick to
    remove), and an "Add an accessory…" select. The select lists each fitting accessory once per
    free mount it could take.
  - Lowering a weapon's quantity drops that copy's accessories.
  - The sheet lists each accessorized copy.
  - Five new strings.
- **Project Overview**: five grouped "Unmodeled real effects" rows.

## Decisions

See the decisions log:
- accessories live on the Resources selection, keyed by weapon copy;
- built-ins occupy mounts, unlike legacy;
- the two printed overrides;
- one of each accessory per copy;
- what stays out: gear inside a scope, underbarrel weapons, and weapon stats.

## Traps

- **Built-ins can't obey the one-per-mount rule.** The Ingram Smartgun X ships with two barrel
  accessories. The second one takes no mount, rather than making the weapon invalid.
- **The data's holster requirement admits melee weapons.** `conceal <= 0` matches a combat knife.
  Only the printed text exposed this.
- **The foci route** (above) was an existing bug. The shared-budget re-validation is easy to get
  subtly wrong at each of its six call sites.
