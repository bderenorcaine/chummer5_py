# WP131 — Armor modifications, Clothing, and the remaining excluded gear

Purchased armor can now take the core armor modifications, within its Capacity. Clothing is sold
at a price the player enters. The rows WP21 left out by cost shape are resolved: the Agent, and two
maglock options, are sold; Custom Commlink and Custom Item are left out as unprinted.

## Research

See the learnings entry. It covers:
- the p. 437 Capacity rule, and the pp. 436/438 tables row by row;
- the chemical-seal, shield and helmet restrictions;
- the Agent's p. 246 prices and the maglock options on p. 447;
- which "excluded" rows turn out not to be printed items at all.

Legacy's mod-category rule is `AllowedCategories = armor.Category + "," + armor.Name` plus
`<addmodcategory>`, with `General` always allowed (`SelectArmorMod`). That is why the 18 `Liner`
rows are unreachable.

## What landed

- **Data**:
  - `data/armor_mods.py`: `load_armor_mods()` returns the 14 installable rows, with Capacity
    (flat or per rating), cost (flat or per rating), `+N`-aware Availability, the required parent
    armor, and a registry note each. `load_armor_mod_slots()` returns each SR5 armor's Capacity,
    Availability, category and `<addmodcategory>`.
  - `data/gear.py`: `Armor.price_range`, `RatingGear.cost_by_rating` / `cost_at`, and three loaders:
    - `load_variable_price_armor()` — Clothing;
    - `load_fixed_values_gear()` — the Agent;
    - `load_maglock_options()` — Card Reader and Anti-Tamper Circuits.
- **Engine**:
  - `engine/armor_mods.py`: `ArmorModPick`, `InstalledArmorMod`, `mod_problem()` (every rule in
    the module docstring), `create_armor_mods()`, and `armor_bonus()`.
  - `engine/resources.py`:
    - `StarterGearItem.cost_by_rating` / `price_range` / `cost_at`;
    - the catalog includes the new rows (380 items);
    - `create_resources_selection(armor_mods=, variable_gear=)`. It also refuses a
      variable-priced name bought without a price.
    - `ResourcesSelection.armor_mods`, `.armor_mod_picks` and `.variable_gear`.
    - `ResourcesAddOns` plus `resources_addon_args()`. These replace WP130's per-site
      `weapon_accessories=` lines, so each carry-forward site has one `**` line.
  - `engine/derived.py`: `worn_armor` adds each copy's mod armor, and counts Clothing once it has
    a rating (`ARMOR_CATEGORIES`). The unmodeled-notes walk visits the mods.
- **Save**: `armor_mods` (`{armor, copy, mod, rating}`) and `variable_gear_picks`
  (`{name, price}`), both additive.
- **Web**:
  - Clothing's row has a price input.
  - The Agent's row lists its price at each offered rating.
  - The Resources panel has an "Armor modifications" block: one fieldset per armor copy that
    can take a mod, with Capacity used, checked boxes for installed mods, and an add select. The
    select lists each mod at each rating that still fits.
  - The sheet lists modified armor with Capacity used.
  - Clothing gets the worn checkbox.
  - Three new strings.
- **Project Overview**: four grouped "Unmodeled real effects" rows.

## Decisions

See the decisions log:
- the add-on bundle;
- 14 of 32 rows offered;
- the printed overrides;
- mod armor feeding worn armor;
- Clothing as a price range;
- the maglock options as line items instead of attached to a parent (a departure from the plan's
  wording);
- the two unprinted rows.

## Traps

- **A `+N` Availability is relative.** Full body armor is 14R, so two of its three printed
  sub-rows are over the cap and never offered. The helmet (Availability "—") is.
- **The Clothing table has no Capacity column.** The data's Feedback Clothing `[3]` would fit
  nowhere it's sold.
- **The existing overspend test bought every catalog name as a flat purchase.** It now skips
  Clothing, which needs a price. Rating items bought that way still price at 0; that looseness
  predates this WP and is left alone.
