# WP132 — Lifestyle options: qualities, roommates, permanent purchase

The primary Lifestyle can now carry p. 370's five Lifestyle Options and be bought permanently
(100 months' upkeep). Roommates were researched and **not built**: they aren't core.

## Research

See the learnings entry: the p. 370 options table and "Buying a Lifestyle". For roommates, legacy's
formula cites Hard Targets p. 139, and the core PDF has no roommate rule at all.

## What landed

- **Data** (`data/lifestyle.py`): `LifestyleOption` and `load_lifestyle_options()`. These are the
  five SR5 Positive/Negative lifestyle `<quality>` rows, with their percent or flat cost,
  repeatability, and a registry note each. The DocWagon contract rows stay out; they are gear.
- **Engine** (`engine/lifestyle.py`):
  - `LifestyleOptionPick`, plus `LifestyleSelection.options` and `.permanent`.
  - `lifestyle_monthly_cost` computes tier × (1 + Σ percent), plus flat amounts, then the
    surcharge.
  - `reprice_lifestyle` keeps the options, and the qualities route's WP113/WP115 repricing now
    uses it.
  - `create_lifestyle_selection(options=, option_catalog=, permanent=)` rejects:
    - options on Street;
    - a repeated non-repeatable option;
    - a Special Work Area without its text;
    - the same work area twice.
  - `PERMANENT_LIFESTYLE_MONTHS = 100`.
- **Derived**: the unmodeled-notes walk visits the options.
- **Save**: `lifestyle_options` and `lifestyle_permanent`, additive. `build_character` resolves the
  options against the loaded catalog.
- **Web**:
  - The lifestyle form has a "Buy permanently" checkbox, a checkbox per option, and three text
    fields for Special Work Areas.
  - The sheet says "Low (bought permanently, …)" and lists the options.
  - Seven new strings.
- **Project Overview**: five "Unmodeled real effects" rows.

## Decisions

See the decisions log: roommates are not built (flagged for the user), percents are summed, the
surcharge applies last, LP is not shown, and a permanent purchase stores 100 months.

## Traps

- **A Jinja inner loop shadows `loop.index0`.** The Special Work Area inputs need the *option's*
  index, captured with `{% set option_index = loop.index0 %}` before the inner loop.
- **Resources A can't afford a permanent Middle lifestyle** (550,000¥ > 450,000¥), so the web test
  uses Low. The sheet prints nuyen without separators ("280000¥").
