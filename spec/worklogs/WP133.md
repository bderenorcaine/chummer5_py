# WP133 — Carryover and starting-nuyen notices

The character sheet now shows three things:
- what nuyen and Karma carry into play: at most 5,000¥ and 7 Karma, with a note for anything that
  will be lost;
- the starting-nuyen roll, taken from the primary Lifestyle.

Nothing changes a spendable number, and the sidebar still shows the true remaining amounts.

## Research

See the learnings entry: p. 94's 5,000¥ rule, p. 95's Starting Nuyen table, James's worked roll on
p. 96, and p. 98's 7-Karma carryover. The plan's question was whether the roll follows the tier, or
the tier after a permanent purchase or roommates. The answer is **the tier**: a permanent purchase
is the same tier, and core has no roommates.

## What landed

- **Data**: `LifestyleTier.starting_dice` / `starting_multiplier`, from `lifestyles.xml`'s
  `<dice>` / `<multiplier>`. All six match p. 95.
- **Engine** (`engine/carryover.py`):
  - `Carryover(remaining, cap)` with `.carried` / `.lost`;
  - `nuyen_carryover()` / `karma_carryover()`;
  - `StartingNuyenRoll` and `starting_nuyen_roll(lifestyle, nuyen_remaining)`.
- **Sheet**:
  - "Carried into play: N¥", plus a loss note when capped, and the roll ("Roll 4D6 × 100¥ for
    starting nuyen when play begins, plus the N¥ carried over") under the nuyen line;
  - "Carried into play: N Karma", plus a loss note, under the Karma line;
  - five new strings.

## Decisions

See the decisions log: display only, a negative remaining carries nothing, the roll follows the
tier, and a fresh character's Karma loss note is expected.

## Traps

- **Templates may not contain a literal ¥, even in a Jinja comment.** A WP102 lint test scans
  template source. Prices go through `| nuyen`.
- **The sheet prints nuyen without thousands separators** ("4000¥"), so tests match that.
