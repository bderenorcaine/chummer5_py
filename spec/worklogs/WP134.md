# WP134 — The Bio page

Phase W's disabled "Bio" nav entry now works. It opens a page with fourteen plain-text fields:
name, street name, player, concept, sex, age, height, weight, eyes, hair, skin, description,
background and notes. The fields persist in saves and are filled by legacy import. The sheet now
heads with the street name and concept.

## Research

See the learnings entry. p. 103 asks backstory questions and prints no field list, so the fields
mirror the legacy `.chum5` elements. All 34 corpus files carry a non-empty `<alias>`.

## What landed

- **Engine** (`engine/biography.py`):
  - the `Biography` dataclass, with `display_name` (street name, else name) and `to_dict()`
    (non-empty fields only);
  - `create_biography()`, which strips text, ignores unknown keys, and caps each field at 20,000
    characters;
  - `LEGACY_ELEMENTS`, `LONG_FIELDS` and `NO_BIOGRAPHY`.
  - `Character.biography`, defaulting to empty.
- **Save**: `biography`, additive. There is no `FORMAT_VERSION` bump; see Decisions.
- **Legacy**: `LegacyCharacter.biography` is parsed from the fourteen elements.
  `import_legacy_character` passes it into the save.
- **Web**:
  - `routes/bio.py`: `POST /ui/bio`. Every change saves, since nothing here can fail but the
    length cap.
  - `_bio.html`: a grid form, with text areas for the three long fields.
  - The shell's `bio` page is enabled, and `chargen_context` merges `_bio_context`.
  - The sheet header shows the display name, then "metatype — concept", then a short list of the
    other filled fields.
  - A little CSS for the form and that list.
  - Three new strings. `Web_Nav_BioUnsupported` is removed.
- **Test changed**: `test_web_shell.py`'s "Resonance and Bio are disabled" became "Resonance is
  disabled"; Bio's page now returns 200.

## Decisions

See the decisions log:
- no format bump;
- the mugshot and GM notes deferred;
- the sheet header's order;
- legacy label keys (so the street name reads "Alias:").

## Traps

- **A priority reconfirmation rebuilds `Character` with `replace()`**, so a new field survives it
  for free. The only fresh `Character(...)` is the very first confirmation, when there's no bio
  yet. A test pins that the bio survives a reconfirmation.
- **The sheet shouldn't repeat the header.** The field list skips the street name, the concept, and
  whichever field is already the display name.
