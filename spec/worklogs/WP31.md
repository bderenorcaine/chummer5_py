# WP31 — Cyberware data loader

Status: complete
Ref: [Implementation Plan.md](../Implementation%20Plan.md) — Phase J, WP31.

## Goal

Load the SR5-core, flat-cost, flat-Essence, Standard-grade cyberware catalog as typed Python
structures, mirroring `chummer5.data.qualities`/`chummer5.data.gear`/`chummer5.data.powers`'s
established "load everything in scope + classify each `<bonus>` node" shape. This is Phase J's
first content WP, following WP30's Essence-tracking prerequisite (`chummer5.engine.derived.essence`)
— WP31 itself produces no `Improvement`s yet (that's WP32's job); it's pure data classification,
mirroring WP3's own independence from WP5.

## Log

### 2026-07-21 — Re-deriving the Plan's pre-stated counts directly from the XML, not assuming them

Parsed `legacy/Chummer/data/cyberware.xml` directly (`xml.etree.ElementTree`) before writing any
loader code, the same "verify, don't assume" habit WP2/WP3/WP4/WP21 established. Confirmed: 362
total `<cyberware>` entries, 87 `source == "SR5"`. Applying a flat-cost filter (bare integer
`<cost>`, mirroring `chummer5.data.gear`'s identical regex) alone gives 62; a flat-Essence filter
(bare, optionally fractional decimal `<ess>`) alone gives 76; both together give exactly **60** —
matching the Implementation Plan's own pre-stated count. The 27 excluded entries carry a
`Rating * N`/`FixedValues(...)`/compound-arithmetic formula in `<cost>` and/or `<ess>` (e.g. Wired
Reflexes' cost `FixedValues(39000,149000,217000)` and Essence `FixedValues(2,3,5)`; Essence
Hole/Antihole's deceptively flat-looking `Rating * 0.01`/`Rating * -0.01`, utility items with a
`rating` range up to 600 that would have silently misparsed as flat Essence costs of `0.01`/`-0.01`
if the classifier only checked `<ess>` was a bare decimal *string* without also confirming the
absence of a `Rating` token). Confirmed no cost/Essence value is negative among the 60, and none of
the 60 carry a `<rating>` tag at all — genuinely single-purchase items, simpler than WP27's powers
(which needed a rating-scaling step despite a flat PP cost). Category distribution of the 60:
Cyberlimb (16), Cyber Implant Weapon (11), Headware (9), Eyeware (7), Bodyware (6), Earware (4),
Cyberlimb Accessory (4), Cyber Implant Weapon Accessory (3) — 8 of the phase intro's 9 named
categories; `Cyberlimb Enhancement` (5 SR5 entries total) has zero flat-cost-and-Essence members, so
it doesn't appear in this WP's own 60, though it remains a real category name for WP34's future
grouping if a later phase widens the filter. No duplicate `id`s or `name`s among the 60 (a single
source file, unlike WP21's three-file gear/armor/weapons collision problem).

### 2026-07-21 — A `<bonus>`-classification wrinkle the Implementation Plan's WP31 entry didn't
fully anticipate: `<disablequality>`-only items

The Plan names `FLAT_BONUS` (Cyberlimb `<conditionmonitor><physical>` items) and calls out that
"two of those also carry `<disablequality>Celerity</disablequality>` — verified inert... document
this as a verified-currently-moot interaction, not a silent gap." Reading every one of the 18
bonus-bearing items' raw `<bonus>` node directly (not assuming the Plan's own framing was
exhaustive) found this undercounts: **6** of the 16 flat Cyberlimb entries carry `<disablequality>
Celerity</disablequality>` — 2 (Obvious/Synthetic Full Leg) alongside a `<conditionmonitor>`
sibling as the Plan describes, but **4 more** (Obvious/Synthetic Foot and Lower Leg) whose *entire*
`<bonus>` is that one `<disablequality>` child, with no `<conditionmonitor>` at all. Verified the
"moot" claim directly rather than trusting the Plan's own assertion: `Celerity` is `source == "RF"`
(excluded from `mvp_quality_catalog` on source grounds alone, independent of anything else) *and*
separately gated behind a `<required><oneof>` of three Changeling-SURGE qualities in the raw
`qualities.xml` — categorically unreachable by any character this engine can build, regardless of
this loader's own scope, so disabling access to it is a real SR5 rule with zero observable
consequence here.

Resolved by stripping `<disablequality>` children from a `<bonus>` node before classifying what
remains (`_VERIFIED_INERT_BONUS_TAGS`), rather than the alternative of name-gating specific items
the way `chummer5.data.qualities._VERIFIED_NO_EFFECT_NAMES` does. Considered both: a name-gate
would need one more moving part than the qualities module's own (a quality is gated by its own
name; here the *gate* is a *value inside* a shared tag, not the cyberware item's own name — 6
different item names all trigger it via the identical child value). Stripping the tag itself before
classification generalizes correctly to any future flat cyberware item that happens to disable a
Celerity-shaped, equally-unreachable quality, without needing a new name added to a list each time —
and, since `Celerity` is the *only* value this tag ever takes among the 60 flat items (checked), it
costs nothing today to implement it this way rather than a name-gate. After stripping: the 2
Full-Leg items reduce to a bare `<conditionmonitor>` (still `FLAT_BONUS`); the 4 Foot/Lower-Leg
items reduce to nothing (`NO_EFFECT`, the same bucket a genuinely empty/absent `<bonus>` lands in).
Wrote a test (`test_full_leg_variants_keep_their_condition_monitor_bonus_despite_disablequality_
sibling`) specifically asserting the Full-Leg pair still resolves to `FLAT_BONUS` — the regression a
naive "any unrecognized child bails to `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED`" classifier (without the stripping step)
would produce, mirroring WP27's own "Attribute Boost trap" test shape.

### 2026-07-21 — Deciding `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` items stay in the loaded catalog, unlike qualities'/
powers' exclude-from-offered-list rule

`chummer5.data.qualities`/`chummer5.data.powers` both exclude their `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` entries from
what a player can actually pick (`mvp_quality_catalog`, the engine-side Adept-power picker) — "never
partially apply an effect" means the whole pick is withheld if any part of it can't be modeled.
Cyberware is different in a way worth making explicit rather than copying that rule by reflex: a
quality's or power's entire *content* is its bonus (nothing else to buy if the bonus can't be
applied), but a cyberware item's fundamental content is its Essence/nuyen cost, with `<bonus>` a
secondary, often-absent effect layered on top — and `chummer5.data.gear` already established (WP21)
that this engine offers gear/armor/weapons with zero engine-side bonus modeling at all, for exactly
this reason. Confirmed against real data this matters concretely: Bone Lacing (Titanium) —
`NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` here (its armor/unarmed-damage bundle isn't modeled) — is a real purchase in
multiple curated mundane fixtures per the Phase J intro, and WP32/33's own acceptance criteria
depend on being able to import/offer it (Essence cost correct, bonus simply not applied and
documented) rather than being told it doesn't exist. So this loader keeps `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` items
in `load_cyberware()`'s output; only `mvp_quality_catalog`/the Adept-power picker's *own* exclusion
behavior was ever about "never partially apply," not a rule that every WP with a `BonusSupport` enum
must repeat identically. Documented explicitly in the module docstring so a future reader doesn't
assume the qualities/powers precedent transfers unchanged.

## Design decisions

- **A new `chummer5.data.cyberware.BonusSupport` Enum, not an import shared with `qualities`/
  `powers`.** Mirrors the Implementation Plan's own framing of this as "the second data module
  needing the identical shape" and asks to decide/document. Chose a fresh Enum per module,
  continuing the precedent `chummer5.data.powers.BonusSupport` already set (a 2-way scheme,
  deliberately omitting `NO_EFFECT`, with its own docstring explaining why) rather than reusing
  `chummer5.data.qualities.BonusSupport` directly — each module's membership is independently
  correct for its own domain (cyberware's `NO_EFFECT` needs no name-gate at all, unlike qualities';
  powers has no `NO_EFFECT` at all), and importing one shared Enum across three unrelated data
  modules would couple them for a savings of one Enum definition.
- **`NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` items stay in `load_cyberware()`'s output** — see Log entry above for the
  full reasoning; this is the one place this WP's design diverges from the qualities/powers
  precedent it otherwise mirrors closely.
- **`<disablequality>` is stripped from a `<bonus>` node before classification, not name-gated per
  item** — see Log entry above.
- **`CyberwareFlatBonus` has no `target_name` field**, unlike `chummer5.data.qualities.FlatBonus`/
  `chummer5.data.powers.PowerFlatBonus` (both of which need one for their skill/attribute-targeting
  bonus kinds). The only `FLAT_BONUS` shape this WP found (`physical_cm`) is, like the Indomitable/
  Improved-Potential Limit bonuses those two modules also carry, a bare per-character total with
  nothing to name — adding an always-`""` field just to match the other two modules' shape would be
  unused scaffolding for a kind of bonus that doesn't need it (the same "add it when a WP actually
  needs one" principle `chummer5.data.powers`'s own docstring already applies to its missing
  `NO_EFFECT` member). A future WP introducing a named-target cyberware bonus (unlikely within the
  current flat-cost scope, since named-target bonuses in the raw data — Smartlink, Damper — are all
  `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` today) can widen the dataclass then.
- **`essence_cost: Decimal`, matching `Improvement.essence_value`'s type (WP30)** — no int/float
  detour; `Decimal(elem.findtext("ess", "0"))` parses the raw XML text directly, since every flat
  `<ess>` value is already a valid decimal literal by construction of the filter itself.

## Implementation

- `src/chummer5/data/cyberware.py` (new): `Cyberware` frozen dataclass (`id`, `name`, `category`,
  `cost: int`, `essence_cost: Decimal`, `source`, `page`, `bonus_support`, `flat_bonuses`,
  `raw_bonus`); `CyberwareFlatBonus` (`target_kind`, `value`); `BonusSupport` Enum (`FLAT_BONUS`,
  `NO_EFFECT`, `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED`); `load_cyberware()` filtering to `source == "SR5"` AND a bare
  integer `<cost>` AND a bare (optionally fractional) decimal `<ess>`; `_classify_bonus` (strips
  `<disablequality>` children, then classifies the remainder); `count_by_support()` mirroring
  `chummer5.data.qualities`/`powers`'s identical helper.

## Outcome

WP31 complete. `chummer5.data.cyberware` loads the SR5-core, flat-cost, flat-Essence, Standard-grade
cyberware catalog (60 of 87 SR5 entries, exactly matching the Implementation Plan's own pre-stated
count) directly from `legacy/Chummer/data/cyberware.xml`. Each item's `<bonus>` node is classified
into `FLAT_BONUS` (8 — the Cyberlimb condition-monitor bonuses, mapping onto the existing
`ImprovementType.PHYSICAL_CM`), `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` (6 — Smartlink, Damper, Spatial Recognizer, and
the 3 Bone Lacing variants, each with a real but unmodeled combat/perception effect), or `NO_EFFECT`
(46 — 42 genuinely bonus-free items plus 4 more whose entire bonus is a verified-moot Celerity
disable). A classification wrinkle beyond what the Implementation Plan's own WP31 entry anticipated
— 4 items whose bonus is *only* the disablequality tag, not the 2 combo cases the Plan named — was
caught by reading every bonus-bearing item's raw XML directly rather than trusting the Plan's count,
and resolved by stripping verified-inert `<disablequality>` children before classification rather
than name-gating specific items. `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` items are deliberately kept in the loaded
catalog (unlike qualities'/powers' exclude-from-offered precedent) since a cyberware item's
Essence/nuyen cost is real, correctly-modeled content on its own even when its bonus isn't — a
considered divergence, documented in the module docstring rather than left implicit. `pytest`/
`ruff`/`mypy` are all green (475 tests, 13 more than before this WP). Phase J continues with WP32
(Cyberware selection & Improvement application), which composes this catalog with WP30's Essence
mechanism into an actual buildable purchase.
