# WP47 — Foci (gear catalog, bonding, and per-category bonuses)

Status: complete
Ref: [Implementation Plan.md](../Implementation%20Plan.md) — Phase M, WP47.

## Goal

Let a magic-active character buy a focus (nuyen, from a new gear-catalog entry) and bond it
(Karma, from the leftover-Karma budget), applying whichever of the 7 focus categories' bonuses
the item grants. Sequenced right after WP46 (Bound Spirits), before the much larger WP48 (Mentor
Spirits), per the Plan's own Phase M ordering.

### 2026-07-22 — Rulebook research first: the Focus Table, the 7-category taxonomy, and the two
different bonded-Force caps

Read the Magic chapter's "Foci"/"Focus Types" section (pp. 317-320) and Step Seven's own
Additional Purchases table (p. 98) via `pdftotext -layout` before writing any code. Confirmed the
Focus Table's bonding-Karma-per-Force values match the Implementation Plan's own WP47 intro
exactly, and confirmed the real trap that intro flagged in advance: the general/lifetime cap
("Magic x 5", p. 317) and the chargen-specific cap ("Magic x 2", p. 98) are both real, and only
the tighter x2 number applies at character creation. Full citations recorded in
`spec/rulebooks/learnings.md`'s new WP47 entry.

### 2026-07-22 — Raw XML survey: 32 SR5 focus entries, not "~15-20" as the Plan's own pre-research
guessed, and every one is Rating-scaled (none are flat-cost)

Direct `xml.etree.ElementTree` parse of `gear.xml`'s `category == "Foci"` entries: 116 total, 32
`source == "SR5"`. Every one of the 32 has `<cost>` = `"Rating * N"` and `<avail>` =
`"(Rating * M)R"` — confirmed uniform, not assumed. This means Foci cannot reuse
`chummer5.data.gear`'s "flat-cost" filter at all (there is nothing flat to find); the loader
instead exposes each item's linear multiplier (`cost_per_force`/`availability_per_force`) and lets
`chummer5.engine.foci` resolve the actual cost against whatever Force the player chooses at
bonding time — exactly the "no general rating-formula resolver needed, just a per-item linear
multiplier" shape the Implementation Plan's own WP47 intro anticipated.

### 2026-07-22 — A real correction to the Plan's own pre-implementation text: Availability can't
be filtered at load time the way bioware's was

The Plan's own WP47 deliverables text says to filter the loader to "Availability ≤ 12," mirroring
WP45's bioware precedent. Checked directly and found this doesn't apply here: bioware's flat
`<avail>` values are bare integers with no `Rating` dependency, so a static per-item cutoff is
well-defined; every focus's Availability scales with the Force the player picks *at chargen time*,
which the loader has no way to know. Moved the Availability check into
`chummer5.engine.foci.create_foci_selection` instead, applied per-pick against the Force actually
chosen (`force * availability_per_force > MAX_AVAILABILITY` is rejected) — the same "exclude
Availability > 12" outcome as the project's 2026-07-21 ruling, just enforced at the one point
where it's a well-formed question. Documented as a correction in both `chummer5.data.foci`'s
module docstring and `spec/rulebooks/learnings.md`, mirroring WP44's own "the Plan's draft wording
was wrong, verified rather than trusted" precedent.

### 2026-07-22 — Per-item `<bonus>` classification: only 2 of 32 items are `FLAT_BONUS`, and why
that's an honest number, not an under-classification

Read every one of the 32 items' raw `<bonus>` node individually (not shape-matched by assumption),
cross-checked against each category's own rulebook prose (p. 317-319). Two verified-inert
selection tags (`<selecttradition/>`, `<selecttext>`) are stripped before classification, mirroring
`chummer5.data.cyberware`'s `<disablequality>`-stripping precedent — both are moot because the
underlying mechanic they describe (Initiation-grade modifiers, Counterspelling/Ritual
Spellcasting/Sustaining/Summoning/Banishing/Binding Tests) has no computed equivalent anywhere in
this engine. Result: 2 `FLAT_BONUS` (Alchemical/Disenchanting Focus, a `<specificskill>` bonus
scaling with the literal `"Rating"` token — mirrors `chummer5.data.powers`' identical "no stored
value, resolved against the purchased pick" shape), 22 `NO_EFFECT` (nothing survives stripping),
8 `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` (Power Focus's blanket MAG-linked-skill bonus, Qi Focus's power-selection
mechanism, the 5 Spellcasting foci's condition-gated bonus — this engine's `Improvement` has no
condition field at all, the same gap Phase K's pre-research flagged for Vision Enhancement — and
Weapon Focus's melee-dice bonus, no combat system modeled). Full per-category breakdown recorded
in `chummer5.data.foci`'s own module docstring.

### 2026-07-22 — Design decision: any magic-active character can bond a focus, not Magician-only
like Bound Spirits

Bound Spirits (WP46) gates on `character.tradition is None` because conjuring specifically is what
makes spirit types offered at all. Foci are different: the rulebook's own "Foci" section (p. 317)
states "you can't bond foci unless you're Awakened" — no Magician-specific requirement, confirmed
directly rather than assumed by analogy to Bound Spirits. `create_foci_selection` gates on
`character.magic_type is not None` instead, and a `test_adept_can_bond_a_focus_not_just_magician`
unit test (plus its web-layer equivalent) confirms an Adept can legally bond an Alchemical Focus.

### 2026-07-22 — Karma budget: Bound Spirits and Foci share one pool with no inherent evaluation
order, but `build_character`'s one-shot reconstruction still needs a fixed one

`chummer5.engine.spirits.provisional_karma_budget` (WP46's own provisional stand-in for Phase O's
not-yet-built ledger) is widened from 2 terms to 4 (`bound_spirit_karma_cost`, `foci_karma_cost`,
both defaulting to 0). Neither `create_bound_spirit_selection` nor `create_foci_selection` chains
into the other's own call the way `create_bioware_selection` takes cyberware's `essence_spent`
directly — each just takes a caller-resolved `karma_budget: int`. This left two genuinely different
correct answers for "who resolves in what order," worked out explicitly rather than picked
arbitrarily:

- **`chummer5.io.save_format.build_character`** (a one-shot reconstruction from scratch) needs a
  fixed order the same reason cyberware/bioware do — Bound Spirits resolves first (unchanged from
  WP46), Foci resolves second, aware of Bound Spirits' own already-computed `karma_cost`. A
  `test_build_character_shares_the_karma_pool_between_bound_spirits_and_foci` test confirms a
  combination that would fit each in isolation is correctly rejected once combined.
- **The web layer's interactive re-validation** (`_spirit_selection_context`/
  `_foci_selection_context`) is symmetric instead, since both selections already have a fixed,
  known cost at render time — each reads the *other's* current `character.X_selection.karma_cost`,
  the same way the nuyen budget's gear/Lifestyle/cyberware/bioware sections already read each
  other's committed spend. This required widening `_spirit_selection_context`'s own signature
  (a new `foci_karma_cost` parameter) even though Bound Spirits itself didn't change — a real,
  if small, retrofit onto WP46's own code.

Full reasoning recorded in `chummer5.engine.foci`'s own module docstring.

### 2026-07-22 — Nuyen: a genuine 5-way shared budget, requiring `foci_cost` threaded through
every existing Step Six section, not just a new one

Unlike Karma (a 2-selection-wide problem before this WP), nuyen was already a 4-way shared budget
(gear/Lifestyle/cyberware/bioware) before Foci existed. Extending it to 5 ways touched
`create_resources_selection` (new `foci_cost` parameter), `_resources_context`,
`_resources_selection_for_display`, `_lifestyle_context`, and `_cyberware_context` — every
existing Step Six section needed a new parameter and an extra term in its own budget arithmetic,
not just the new `_foci_selection_context`. Caught one real bug while doing this: `submit_cyberware`
itself never accounted for Foci's own committed nuyen cost in its `nuyen_available` calculation
before this WP's edit — a pre-existing gap this WP's own symmetric-wiring discipline surfaced and
fixed as part of wiring Foci in, not a new regression.

### 2026-07-22 — A UI simplification, deliberately not exposing the engine's full flexibility: at
most one bonded pick per catalog item in the web form

`create_foci_selection` allows bonding two copies of the same catalog item (at possibly different
Force) — no rulebook rule forbids it. The web picker (`_parse_foci`) only supports one Force value
per catalog item (`force_focus_{id}`, 0 meaning "not bonded"), the same simplification the
Implementation Plan's own precedent set for e.g. gear quantity pickers, since a "how many, at what
Force each" second dimension isn't needed by any real fixture and no other section's UI has
needed one either. Documented in `_parse_foci`'s own docstring.

### 2026-07-22 — A real bug caught by the web-level round-trip test: `POST /character/load` never
threaded the `FOCI` catalog into `build_character`

`test_save_load_round_trip_preserves_foci` initially failed with "not a Foci catalog id" on the
*load* side, even though the save/engine-level tests all passed — traced to
`chummer5.web.app.load_save`'s own `build_character(...)` call, which never gained a `foci=FOCI`
keyword argument. The same class of gap WP46's own worklog flagged for `bound_spirit_picks`
threading (one function has a read path and a write path, and only exercising one in isolation
doesn't prove the other) — caught here by the same discipline, a genuine save/load *round-trip*
test rather than two one-directional ones.

## Design decisions

- **New module, `chummer5.data.foci` + `chummer5.engine.foci`, not folded into `gear.py`/`magic.py`
  respectively.** Mirrors WP46's own "own module" precedent for the data layer (Foci's cost/
  Availability/category shape is nothing like `chummer5.data.gear`'s flat-cost items) and for the
  engine layer (`magic.py` already covers Magic type/rating/tradition/free-skill-grant/spells/
  powers; Foci is its own self-contained SR5 chargen decision).
- **`focus_category` is derived from the item's own name, not a `gear.xml` field** (none exists) —
  a hardcoded per-name table for the 12 non-Spell names, plus "contains a comma" as the sole
  discriminator for the 20 uniformly-shaped Spell-category names. Verified individually against
  the rulebook's own per-category prose, not assumed uniform.
- **`FOCUS_MAX_RATING = 18`** (the raw XML `<rating>` field, confirmed uniform across all 32
  items) is enforced as a data-integrity bound even though it never actually binds in practice
  (the chargen-specific Force cap — Magic x 2, max 12 for Magic 6 — always binds first). Kept for
  the same "correct even if redundant" reason `chummer5.engine.magic.create_power_selection`
  enforces `power.limit` alongside the Power Point budget.
- **`FociSelection` wraps `picks` alongside both budgets and both spent totals**, mirroring
  `BoundSpiritSelection`'s identical shape, for the same "avoid recomputing on every render"
  reason.
- **Duplicate purchases of the same catalog item are allowed** (no dedup check) — no rulebook rule
  forbids bonding two Weapon Foci, mirroring cyberware/bioware/gear's existing "repeats allowed"
  default; the *web* picker doesn't expose this (see above), but the engine-level function does.
- **`<gear>`/bonded-foci import from legacy `.chum5` is deliberately not implemented**, mirroring
  WP46's identical "flagged, not scheduled" treatment for `<spirits>` — `chummer5.io.legacy_import`
  never parses `<gears>` at all (a pre-existing, already-documented gap for ordinary Step Six gear
  too), so `foci_picks` simply defaults to `()` on every legacy import, the same way
  `bound_spirit_picks` already does.

## Outcome

WP47 complete. `chummer5.data.foci`/`chummer5.engine.foci` add Foci as a Rating-scaled catalog
(32 SR5 items across 7 rulebook categories) with a real chargen-specific bonded-Force cap (Magic x
2, tighter than the general Magic x 5 rule) enforced alongside a count cap (≤ Magic) and a
per-pick Availability cap (≤ 12, applied at the chosen Force rather than statically — a genuine
structural difference from bioware's own Availability filter, corrected from the Implementation
Plan's own draft wording). Only 2 of 32 items (Alchemical/Disenchanting Focus) produce a real,
applicable `FLAT_BONUS` skill Improvement; the other 30 are honestly classified `NO_EFFECT`/
`NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` after individually verifying their real rulebook effect targets a mechanic
this engine has no computed equivalent for. Foci draws from the same shared nuyen pool as gear/
Lifestyle/cyberware/bioware (now a genuine 5-way check) and the same provisional leftover-Karma
pool as Bound Spirits (`chummer5.engine.spirits.provisional_karma_budget` widened from 2 terms to
4) — save/load and the web layer each resolve the shared Karma budget correctly, using a fixed
evaluation order for the one-shot `build_character` reconstruction and symmetric mutual awareness
for the web layer's independent interactive re-validation. As anticipated, no real fixture in the
corpus exercises Foci at all — every test here is synthetic/hand-verified against the rulebook and
`gear.xml` directly, the same honesty standard WP25/WP46 already established.
`pytest`/`ruff`/`mypy` are all green (720 tests total: 11 in `test_foci.py`, 20 in
`test_foci_selection.py`, 9 in `test_web_foci.py`, plus 8 new cases folded into
`test_save_format.py`). Phase M continues with WP48 (Mentor Spirits) next, per the Plan's own
ordering, sized like WP22/WP27 — sequenced last within the phase deliberately so it doesn't block
WP46/WP47 from shipping first.
