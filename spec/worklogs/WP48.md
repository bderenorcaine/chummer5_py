# WP48 — Mentor Spirits

Status: complete
Ref: [Implementation Plan.md](../Implementation%20Plan.md) — Phase M, WP48 (last WP in the phase).

## Goal

Let a Magic-active character take the Mentor Spirit quality and choose a specific archetype, each
granting up to 3 distinct bonuses (General, Magician-only, Adept-only) per the rulebook. Sized like
WP22 (qualities)/WP27 (powers) — a classify-every-entry pass over a real SR5 catalog — not a quick
add-on, per the Plan's own framing. Sequenced last within Phase M so it doesn't block WP46/WP47.

## Log

### 2026-07-22/23 — Rulebook research: 16 SR5 archetypes (not the Plan's own "20+" estimate), and
the "3 advantages" model confirmed rather than corrected

Read the Magic chapter's "Mentor Spirits"/"Mentor Spirit Archetypes" section (pp. 320-324) via
`pdftotext -layout` before writing any code. Direct `xml.etree.ElementTree` parse of
`mentors.xml`: 81 total `<mentor>` entries, only 16 `source == "SR5"` — a real correction to the
Plan's own pre-research "20+" guess, the same kind of "verify, don't trust the draft estimate"
finding WP47 made for Foci's own count. Unlike WP44/WP47, though, the rulebook's own text
*confirmed* the Plan's "3 distinct bonuses (general, magician-only, adept-only)" framing exactly
(p. 320: "Each archetype offers three advantages..."), rather than correcting it.

A real wrinkle surfaced during the raw-XML survey looked at first like a 4th slot: 3 archetypes
(Cat, Fire-Bringer, Wise Warrior) encode their General bonus as 2 `<choice>` elements instead of a
fixed top-level `<bonus>`. Checked against the rulebook text directly and confirmed this is *not*
a 4th slot — it's the General ("All") slot itself phrased as a binary player choice (e.g. Cat,
p. 321: "All: +2 dice to either Gymnastics or Sneaking Tests (choose one)"). Also confirmed via
`SelectMentorSpirit.cs` (`lstMentor_SelectedIndexChanged`): the Magician/Adept slots are gated
purely by a `"Magician: "`/`"Adept: "` name prefix, filtered against
`Character.MagicianEnabled`/`AdeptEnabled` — the `set="2"` XML attribute some choices carry is
UI-dropdown-layout-only, never consulted for gating. `chummer5.data.mentors` therefore derives
gating from the name prefix alone and never reads `set` at all. Full citations and the complete
per-archetype bonus-shape breakdown recorded in `spec/rulebooks/learnings.md`'s new WP48 entry.

### 2026-07-23 — Per-slot classification: 17 of 51 classifiable bonus slots are `FLAT_BONUS`, 1 is
a verified `NO_EFFECT`, the rest `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` — every Adept choice included

Classified every General/Magician/Adept slot individually (mirroring WP22/27/47's per-entry
discipline), reusing only the `<specificskill>` flat-skill shape `chummer5.data.qualities`/
`chummer5.data.foci` already recognize (the only flat shape that appears anywhere in this file).
Result: 10 fixed General bonuses + Mountain's Magician choice (the one `<specificskill>` Magician
slot with no `<condition>` child) + 6 sub-options across Cat/Fire-Bringer/Wise Warrior's General
choice-pairs = 17 `FLAT_BONUS`. Rat's Magician choice ("+2 dice to Alchemy tests when harvesting
reagents...", p. 323) has **no `<bonus>` element at all** in the raw XML — confirmed this is the
legacy data authors' own deliberate choice (harvesting isn't a tracked dice pool), not an
extraction gap, and classified it `NO_EFFECT` via a name-gated allowlist mirroring
`chummer5.data.qualities`'s `_VERIFIED_NO_EFFECT_NAMES` precedent. Every Adept-slot choice
(`<specificpower>`, granting free Power levels **outside** the normal Power Point budget) is
classified `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` uniformly, even the mechanically simplest single-`<specificpower>`
cases — a new "grant a Power for free" shape this engine has never built, mirroring
`chummer5.data.foci`'s own "no computed equivalent, not 'the shape looks simple'" bar for Qi/Power
Focus. Every other Magician choice is `<spellcategory>` (a per-category spellcasting dice pool
this engine never computes, since it resolves no in-play Tests at all) or a `<specificskill>` with
an extra `<condition>` child (Eagle/Sea/Thunderbird's Summoning bonuses) — both
`NOT_YET_SUPPORTED`. 3 General bonuses fail classification outright: Bear's `<damageresistance>`
(an in-play resistance-test bonus), Dragonslayer's `<selectskill skillcategory=...>` (a
pick-any-skill mechanism with no fixed target), and Eagle's compound `<addqualities>` +
`<specificskill>` node (fails as a whole since `addqualities` — which silently grants Allergy
(pollutants, mild) per the rulebook's own Disadvantages text — isn't a recognized shape, and this
project's "all children must be recognized" rule never partially applies a compound bonus).

### 2026-07-23 — Design decision: every archetype is offered regardless of classification,
mirroring Foci's precedent rather than Qualities'

`chummer5.data.qualities` excludes a `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` quality from the offered list entirely
("never partially apply"). Mentor Spirit archetypes take the opposite, `chummer5.data.foci`-style
posture instead: all 16 are offered regardless of classification, and only the classified-
`FLAT_BONUS` slots are actually applied as `Improvement`s. This is deliberate, not an
inconsistency — the primary player-facing decision here is picking an archetype for its narrative
advantage/disadvantage identity (exactly Foci's own reasoning: "the pick is real even when the
bonus isn't applied"), not shopping a purely mechanical catalog the way the Quality picker is.
Two archetypes (Bear, Eagle) currently apply nothing at all when picked by an Adept (both their
General bonus *and* their Adept choice are `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED`) — an honest, documented gap, not
a bug, the same "verified, not silently wrong" posture WP47 accepted for 30 of 32 foci.

### 2026-07-23 — A genuinely new shape: Mentor Spirit is a Quality requiring a catalog-backed
sub-selection, not a flat bonus or a free-text field

The Mentor Spirit quality itself (`qualities.xml` id `ced3fecf-2277-4b20-b1e0-894162ca9ae2`, 5
Karma, Positive, `<bonus><selectmentorspirit /></bonus>`) was already correctly excluded from
WP22's offered catalog (`NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` — a placeholder tag with no flat shape). Rather than
widening `chummer5.data.qualities`'s general classifier to somehow recognize
`<selectmentorspirit />`, `chummer5.engine.qualities` handles it as a named special case: `offerable_
quality_catalog`/`create_quality_selection` both widen via a new shared helper,
`_add_mentor_spirit_if_eligible`, gated on `magic_type in ("Magician", "Adept")` (narrowing the
real quality's `<required><oneof>` gate — Adept/Aspected Magician/Aware/Enchanter/Explorer/
Apprentice/Magician/Mystic Adept — to the two types Phase I actually produces).
`create_quality_selection` gained `magic_type`/`mentor_catalog`/`mentor_spirit_archetype_id`/
`mentor_spirit_general_choice_id` parameters and resolves a `MentorSpiritPick` (archetype, chosen
General option, auto-resolved Magician-or-Adept option) whenever "Mentor Spirit" is among the
selected quality ids — validating the archetype id, requiring an explicit General choice only
when the archetype offers more than one option, and rejecting an orphaned archetype pick with no
quality backing it. `QualitySelection` gained a `mentor_spirit: MentorSpiritPick | None = None`
field. Karma flows through the *ordinary* `positive_karma` sum (Mentor Spirit is a normal 5-Karma
Positive Quality, checked against `QUALITY_KARMA_LIMIT` like any other) — unlike Bound Spirits/
Foci, there is no separate provisional-budget concern here at all.

A new `ImprovementSource.MENTOR_SPIRIT` (9th member) and `apply_mentor_spirit_improvements`
(applies `pick.general_choice`/`pick.gated_choice`'s `FLAT_BONUS` slots, keyed by the archetype's
own id) keep the archetype's bonuses independently retractable from the Mentor Spirit quality's
own (always-empty) `QUALITY`-sourced Improvements.

### 2026-07-23 — Web layer: reusing the existing quality form's `hx-trigger="change"` instead of a
new fragment endpoint, plus two real bugs the round-trip/talent-change tests caught

The archetype/General-choice picker was added directly inside `_quality_selection.html`'s
existing `<form hx-trigger="change">` rather than a new HTMX fragment endpoint (the pattern
`_talent_options.html`/`/ui/talent-options` established) — since the whole form already
re-submits and re-renders on any field change, changing the archetype `<select>` naturally
cascades into a fresh render of the General-choice sub-list for the newly-selected archetype, with
no extra route needed. A rough edge, documented rather than polished away given the time budget
and that no fixture exercises this: switching archetypes with a stale General-choice value
produces a real validation error on that one submission (clearing correctly on the next), rather
than silently resetting the dropdown client-side — mirrors WP47's own "at most one bonded pick per
catalog item" deliberate UI simplification.

`submit_qualities` normalizes the archetype/General-choice form fields to `None` whenever the
Mentor Spirit checkbox itself isn't checked, so unchecking it never requires the player to also
clear a stale dropdown (`create_quality_selection`'s "orphaned pick" guard is meant for a
tampered/stale request, not this ordinary UI interaction).

Talent changes needed a new, surgical helper: unlike spell/power/spirit/Foci selections (each
reset to `None` wholesale by `_replace_committed_talent` on any Talent (re)confirmation),
`quality_selection` as a whole can't be reset that way — it also holds every magic-*independent*
quality the player picked (Catlike, Indomitable, ...), which a Talent change has no reason to
discard. `_strip_mentor_spirit_selection` drops just the Mentor Spirit quality + archetype pick
(whose `gated_choice` is Talent-dependent, and which stops being offered at all once `magic_type`
becomes `None`), keeping every other quality intact — applied unconditionally on any Talent
(re)confirmation, matching spells/powers/spirits/foci's own existing "reset on every submission,
not just an actual diff" behavior.

Two real bugs caught by tests, not assumed away:
1. `_character_save_from_session` initially persisted `mentor_spirit_general_choice_id` for
   *every* archetype pick, including single-option ones (whose synthetic `"{id}:general"` choice
   id was never meant to be a real player-submitted value). Reloading such a save then hit
   `create_quality_selection`'s original "must be `None` for a single-option archetype" rule and
   failed with "Dog offers no General bonus choice to make" — caught by
   `test_save_load_round_trip_preserves_mentor_spirit`. Fixed by relaxing the engine-layer rule
   instead of special-casing the save layer: `mentor_spirit_general_choice_id`, if given, now just
   has to match one of the archetype's own `general_options` (whether there are one or two) —
   simpler *and* more correct than the original asymmetric rule, since a save naturally round-trips
   whatever `general_choice.id` was actually resolved regardless of how many options existed.
2. `POST /character/load`'s `build_character(...)` call never gained a `mentors=MENTORS` keyword
   argument — the exact same class of gap WP47's own worklog flagged (a function has a read path
   and a write path; exercising only one in isolation doesn't prove the other) — caught by the
   same round-trip test once bug 1 was fixed underneath it.

## Design decisions

- **`chummer5.data.mentors` is a new module**, mirroring `chummer5.data.foci`'s "fresh, minimal
  `BonusSupport` enum" precedent rather than reusing `chummer5.data.qualities`'s (only the flat
  `<specificskill>` shape ever appears here).
- **`MentorSpiritArchetype.general_options` is a tuple of 1 or 2 `MentorChoice`s, not a separate
  "is this a real choice" flag** — `len(general_options) > 1` is exactly the condition under which
  a player must pick one, consumed directly by `create_quality_selection` rather than re-derived.
- **The archetype pick lives inside `QualitySelection` (`.mentor_spirit`), not a new `Character`
  field** — follows the Implementation Plan's own explicit framing ("extend the Mentor Spirit
  quality's application to require an archetype selection"), unlike Lifestyle (WP20) or Bound
  Spirits/Foci (WP46/47), each of which got its own top-level `Character` field. Mentor Spirit is
  fundamentally a Quality-shaped decision (5 Karma, checked against the ordinary Positive-Quality
  cap), not a sibling chargen stage.
- **`gated_choice(archetype, magic_type)` lives in `chummer5.data.mentors`, not
  `chummer5.engine.qualities`** — a pure data-layer lookup (Magician vs. Adept slot), reused
  identically by both the engine (`create_quality_selection`) and would-be future callers with no
  engine dependency.
- **Every archetype offered regardless of classification** (see Log) — a deliberate Foci-style
  posture, not Qualities'.
- **`ImprovementSource.MENTOR_SPIRIT` is its own 9th member**, not folded into `QUALITY` — keeps
  the archetype's bonuses independently retractable from the quality's own (always-empty) ones,
  the same reasoning `FOCUS` got its own member in WP47 rather than reusing `CYBERWARE`/`BIOWARE`.
- **Legacy `.chum5` import of a real Mentor Spirit pick stays unimplemented, documented rather
  than silently gapped** — `LegacyCharacter` has no `<mentorspirit>` parsing, so a real pick
  continues to land in the ordinary `dropped_qualities` excluded-and-reported path (unchanged
  behavior; only a docstring note was added) — mirrors WP46/47's identical "flagged, not
  scheduled" treatment for `<spirits>`/bonded foci.

## Outcome

WP48 complete — Phase M (Magic expansion: Bound Spirits, Foci, Mentor Spirits) is now fully
shipped. `chummer5.data.mentors`/`chummer5.engine.qualities` add Mentor Spirits as a
16-SR5-archetype catalog (not the Plan's own pre-research "20+" guess), each with 1-2 General
bonus options plus an auto-resolved Magician/Adept slot, individually classified against the real
`<bonus>` shapes: 17 of 51 classifiable slots are `FLAT_BONUS` and applied as real `Improvement`s,
1 is a verified `NO_EFFECT` (Rat's harvesting-reagents Magician choice, confirmed via the legacy
data's own deliberate omission), the rest honestly `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` (uniformly for every
Adept-slot free-Power grant, and for General/Magician bonuses targeting in-play tests or
selection mechanisms this engine's combat-free, Test-free scope doesn't compute). Every archetype
is offered regardless of classification, mirroring Foci's "the pick is real even when the bonus
isn't" precedent — including two archetypes (Bear, Eagle) that currently apply zero computed bonus
to an Adept, an honest, documented gap rather than a silently wrong one. Mentor Spirit's 5-Karma
cost flows through the ordinary Positive-Quality karma cap, not a separate budget, since — unlike
Bound Spirits/Foci — that machinery already existed and needed no new ledger. As anticipated, no
real fixture in the corpus exercises this at all (confirmed in Phase M's own intro); every test
here is synthetic/hand-verified against the rulebook and `mentors.xml` directly, the same honesty
standard WP25/WP46/WP47 already established. Two real bugs (an engine-layer validation rule too
strict for its own save-format round trip; a missing `mentors=MENTORS` keyword on the load route)
were caught by the round-trip test itself, the same class of gap WP47's own worklog flagged and
the reason this project keeps writing genuine round-trip tests rather than one-directional ones.
`pytest`/`ruff`/`mypy` are all green (769 tests total: 17 in `test_mentors.py`, 10 in
`test_web_mentor_spirit.py`, plus 22 and 8 new cases folded into `test_quality_selection.py`/
`test_save_format.py` respectively).
