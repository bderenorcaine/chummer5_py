# WP55 — SINner and the other selecttext-blocked-but-policy-eligible qualities

Status: complete
Ref: [Implementation Plan.md](../Implementation%20Plan.md) — Phase O, WP55 (depends on WP52's
concrete motivating case and WP22's own selecttext research).

## Goal

Offer SINner (all 4 variants) plus 5 other SR5 qualities WP22's own research had already found
blocked by an identical, single missing mechanism — a free-text ("select a target") detail the
player fills in — rather than by any unmodeled *computed* effect: Code of Honor, Codeblock, Social
Stress, Natural Immunity (Natural)/(Synthetic). Building the mechanism was motivated by a concrete,
real gap: `Wesson.chum5`'s own `SINner (Corporate Limited)` pick is exactly what makes his real
Karma-funded EDG purchase (WP52) affordable, and this project's inability to represent it was the
documented reason his `import_chum5()` still didn't reach a full end-to-end success after WP52.

## Log

### 2026-07-24 — Rulebook research: re-extracting all 9 qualities' citations directly, not trusting
WP22's own paraphrase

Extracted via `pdftotext -f 78 -l 87 -layout` (printed pp. 76-85, the established WP22/WP52/WP53
footer offset). Confirmed exact karma values (SINner: National 5, Criminal 10, Corporate Limited
15, Corporate 25; Code of Honor 15; Codeblock 10; Social Stress 8; Natural Immunity 4/10) and that
every one of the 9 is narrative/out-of-scope rather than a chargen-time computed value — full
citations in `spec/rulebooks/learnings.md`'s new WP55 entry. One real, data-only (not
rulebook-prose) finding: SINner (Criminal)'s own `<bonus>` carries `<notoriety>1</notoriety>`
alongside `<selecttext/>` — a second child the classifier has to tolerate without needing to parse
it, recorded as an unmodeled effect (Project Overview's registry) rather than silently dropped.

### 2026-07-24 — Classifying by name, not by structurally parsing `<selecttext>`'s own attributes

Two of the 9 (`SINner (Corporate)`/`SINner (Corporate Limited)`) carry extra `xml`/`xpath`/
`allowedit` attributes on their `<selecttext>` node (autocomplete-list hints for the legacy C# UI,
confirmed by inspecting `lifemodules.xml`'s referenced macros) — the other 7 have a bare
`<selecttext/>`. Rather than teaching `_classify_bonus` to recognize `<selecttext>` structurally
(with or without attributes) as its own case, classification is name-gated
(`_VERIFIED_SELECT_TEXT_NAMES`), the identical pattern `NO_EFFECT` (WP22) already established —
this sidesteps needing any attribute-parsing logic at all, since the attributes carry no computed
value regardless.

### 2026-07-24 — `Quality.forbidden_names`: parsed generally, confirmed safe to generalize by
checking every SR5 quality's `<forbidden>` shape first

Before writing the parser, checked all 31 SR5 qualities carrying a `<forbidden>` block: every single
one is exactly `<forbidden><oneof><quality>...</quality>...</oneof></forbidden>`, no other shape
anywhere in the SR5-sourced subset. This is what justified parsing it as a new `Quality.
forbidden_names` field for *every* quality (not scoped narrowly to this WP's own 9) — see
`chummer5.data.qualities`'s module docstring for the reasoning. Net effect: WP22's own
"deliberately deferred" mutually-exclusive pairs (Insomnia (Basic)/(Full); Distinctive Style/
Blandness) get correct mutual-exclusion enforcement for free the moment a future WP offers either,
with no changes needed to `create_quality_selection` itself.

### 2026-07-24 — Mutual exclusion decided as a general check, not scoped narrowly to SINner

Per the Plan's own "decide and document" instruction: built one general check in
`create_quality_selection` (for each selected quality, none of its `forbidden_names` may name
another *selected* quality's name) rather than a SINner-specific 4-way check. This is what makes
`Quality.forbidden_names`'s general parse (above) actually pay off immediately, not just
theoretically — the identical check enforces SINner's 4-way exclusivity and Natural Immunity's
exclusion against the not-yet-offered Weak Immune System with no special-casing either way.

### 2026-07-24 — Attempting Wesson's end-to-end import: succeeds for the first time

`Wesson.chum5`'s real `SINner (Corporate Limited)` (`<extra>Lone Star Security Services</extra>`)
now resolves into `quality_text` via the exact same `LegacyQuality.extra` field `legacy_chum5.py`
already parsed (WP23) — no new parsing needed, just reading a field that existed all along.
Confirmed the arithmetic matches WP52's own prediction exactly: `character.karma_remaining` before
the EDG purchase is now 30 (`25 - 10` Guts `+ 15` SINner), the 20-Karma purchase leaves 10. This is
the first WP to get `import_chum5(Wesson.chum5)` to a real `Character`, after WP28 (blocked on
Talent/tradition scope, since fixed) and WP52 (blocked on exactly this gap) both fell short —
replaced `test_wesson_still_blocked_after_wp52_by_the_excluded_sinner_qualitys_lost_karma_credit`
with `test_wesson_imports_end_to_end` in `tests/integration/test_magic_fixtures.py`.

## Design decisions

- **`BonusSupport.SELECT_TEXT`, name-gated (`_VERIFIED_SELECT_TEXT_NAMES`)** — mirrors `NO_EFFECT`'s
  existing WP22 pattern exactly; `_classify_bonus` checks it before attempting to structurally parse
  the `<bonus>` node's children at all.
- **`Quality.forbidden_names: tuple[str, ...] = ()`**, parsed generally from every SR5 quality's
  `<forbidden><oneof><quality>` block, not scoped to this WP's own 9 — see the Log's own reasoning
  for why this was confirmed safe (a uniform shape across all 31 qualities that have one).
- **Mutual exclusion is a general check** in `create_quality_selection` (checked by *name*, not id,
  since a forbidden quality need not itself be offered for the check to matter — Natural Immunity's
  own exclusion against the not-yet-offered Weak Immune System is the concrete case this matters
  for), not scoped narrowly to SINner.
- **`QualitySelection.quality_text: MappingProxyType[str, str] = MappingProxyType({})`** —
  `{quality_id: free_text}`, populated for exactly the selected `SELECT_TEXT` qualities.
  `create_quality_selection` gains a `quality_text` parameter, validating both directions: every
  selected `SELECT_TEXT` quality needs a non-blank entry, and no entry may be supplied for anything
  else (an orphaned/tampered field) — mirroring the existing Mentor Spirit archetype-pick validation
  style exactly.
- **Overflow/orphan validation, not a richer per-quality schema.** A `SELECT_TEXT` quality's free
  text is a plain string, stored and displayed, nothing more — no attempt to structurally validate
  *what* the text says (e.g. "is this a real SIN-issuing nation") the way the legacy C#'s `xpath`
  autocomplete hints suggest but don't enforce either.
- **`CharacterSave.quality_text`** mirrors `specializations`'/`karma_attribute_points`'s own
  `MappingProxyType[str, str]`/`[str, int]` shape — no richer type needed.
- **Legacy import**: no new blocking check, no new dropped-quality category — the 9 qualities simply
  become part of `mvp_quality_catalog`'s existing offered set, and the only new step is resolving
  each `SELECT_TEXT` quality's own `<extra>` into `quality_text` inside the loop that already builds
  `quality_ids`. A blank `<extra>` (unreached by any curated fixture) is deliberately left as a plain
  `ValueError` bubbling out of `build_character`, the same fallback this module already documents for
  other real-rules-violations its itemized checks don't anticipate.
- **Web layer**: one new `<input type="text">` cell per row, gated on `row.needs_text`
  (`quality.bonus_support == SELECT_TEXT`) — no new route, no new template pattern. The character
  sheet shows the chosen text inline (`"SINner (National) (UCAS)"`) rather than adding a new context
  key/template branch.

## Implementation

- `src/chummer5/data/qualities.py`: `BonusSupport.SELECT_TEXT` (new); `_VERIFIED_SELECT_TEXT_NAMES`
  (9 names); `_classify_bonus` checks it before generic parsing; `Quality.forbidden_names: tuple[str,
  ...] = ()` (new field) + `_parse_forbidden_names` (new, general `<forbidden><oneof><quality>`
  parser).
- `src/chummer5/engine/qualities.py`: `_OFFERABLE_SUPPORT` widened with `SELECT_TEXT`;
  `QualitySelection.quality_text` (new field); `create_quality_selection` gains a `quality_text`
  parameter and validates (a) mutual exclusion via `forbidden_names`, (b) every selected
  `SELECT_TEXT` quality has non-blank text, (c) no orphaned text entries.
- `src/chummer5/io/save_format.py`: `CharacterSave.quality_text: MappingProxyType[str, str] =
  MappingProxyType({})` (new field), threaded through `create_character_save`/`_save_to_dict`/
  `_dict_to_save`/`build_character`'s `create_quality_selection` call.
- `src/chummer5/io/legacy_import.py`: the quality-resolution loop now also builds a `quality_text`
  dict, reading `legacy_quality.extra` for any offered `SELECT_TEXT` quality; threaded into
  `create_character_save`.
- `src/chummer5/web/app.py`: `_quality_selection_context` gains a `quality_text` parameter and
  `needs_text`/`text_value` per row; `submit_qualities` parses `quality_text_{id}` fields for
  checked `SELECT_TEXT` rows and passes them through; `_strip_mentor_spirit_selection` carries
  `quality_text` forward when rebuilding around a Talent change; the save/download builder and
  `_character_sheet_context`'s `quality_names` thread/display it too.
- `src/chummer5/web/templates/_quality_selection.html`: new "Detail" column with a text input for
  `needs_text` rows; form's `hx-trigger` widened to debounce on `input` (mirrors `_contacts.html`'s
  existing free-text-field precedent).
