# WP66 — Rating-priced starter gear (fake SINs, AR glasses/goggles/contacts, earbuds/headphones, …)

Status: complete
Ref: [Implementation Plan.md](../Implementation%20Plan.md) — Phase U, WP66 (the last Phase U package).

## Goal

Close a real, previously-documented content gap: the starter-gear catalog offered only **flat-cost**
SR5 items, silently excluding the entire class of `Rating * N`-priced gear a mundane runner routinely
needs — fake SINs, AR glasses/goggles/contacts, earbuds/headphones, medkits, and the rest. This was a
deliberate WP21 MVP cut (its docstring flagged "120 `Rating`-formula excluded"), not a regression, but
the excluded set is common enough to lift now. The one non-trivial WP of Phase U: it adds a per-item
**rating dimension** the flat-cost model didn't have, plus the first enforcement of the project's
2026-07-21 Availability ≤ 12 line in the gear catalog.

## What I did

### Data layer (`chummer5.data.gear`)

- `load_rating_gear()` — the deliberate complement to `load_gear`'s flat-cost filter over the *same*
  `gear.xml`: the 120 SR5-sourced entries whose `<cost>` is `Rating * N`. Returns `RatingGear`
  (`cost_per_rating`, `max_rating` from `<rating>`, raw `availability` string). A literal XML→dataclass
  pass like the other loaders — no policy.
- `parse_rating_availability(avail)` — turns a Rating-scaled `<avail>` into `(slope, intercept)` so
  availability at a rating is `slope*rating + intercept`. Strips the trailing `R`/`F` legality marker
  (only magnitude matters for the ≤12 cap), strips surrounding parens, handles every SR5 form seen
  (`0`, `12F`, `Rating`, `(Rating)R`, `Rating * N`, `(Rating * N)F`, `(Rating + N)R`). Returns `None`
  for a `+`-prefixed modifier (an accessory added to a parent's avail — excluded, mirroring
  `load_gear`'s `+`-cost accessory exclusion) or any unrecognized form (fail-closed).

### Engine layer (`chummer5.engine.resources`)

- `StarterGearItem` gained `cost_per_rating`/`max_rating` (set on a rating-priced *catalog* entry, with
  `cost = 0` — no price until a rating is chosen) and `rating` (set on a *purchased* item, whose `cost`
  is then `rating * cost_per_rating`). All default `None`, so flat items and every pre-WP66 caller are
  unchanged.
- `build_starter_gear_catalog` gained a `rating_gear` parameter. Three policy filters live here (not in
  the loader): **Foci/Formulae categories dropped** (owned by the dedicated Foci bonding system);
  **Availability ≤ 12** via `_availability_capped_max_rating` (clamps each item's selectable rating to
  where `<avail>` stays ≤ 12, drops an item over the cap even at rating 1 like Plastic Explosive `16F`);
  and the **flat/rating `Camera` name collision** resolved in favour of the real rating item (`_consider_
  rating` lets a rating item displace a same-named 0-cost flat component). 120 loaded → 51 offered
  (64 Foci/Formulae, 4 `+`-modifiers, 1 over-cap dropped).
- `create_resources_selection` gained a keyword-only `rating_gear: Iterable[tuple[str, int]]` — each
  `(name, rating)` becomes a purchased `StarterGearItem` (validated: must be a rating-priced catalog
  item, `1 <= rating <= max_rating`), repeats allowed for quantity, counted against the same nuyen
  budget as flat gear.
- `resources_gear_args(selection)` — new helper splitting a committed `ResourcesSelection` back into
  the `(gear_names, rating_gear)` argument pair, so carry-forward re-validation preserves each rating
  item's chosen rating instead of collapsing it to a bare name.

### Save format (`chummer5.io.save_format`)

- `CharacterSave.rating_gear_picks: tuple[(name, rating), …]` (defaults `()`, "predates this field"),
  serialized in `_save_to_dict`/`_dict_to_save`, threaded into all three `build_character`
  `create_resources_selection` calls.

### Web layer

- `routes/resources.py`: `_parse_rating_gear` (quantity + rating form fields → `rating_gear`),
  `_resources_context` builds per-row `is_rating`/`rating`/`max_rating`/`unit_cost`/`subtotal`, and
  `submit_resources` threads `rating_gear` through validation and re-render.
- `_resources.html`: a `Rating` column with a `<select>` (1..capped `max_rating`) for rating-priced rows;
  flat rows show `—`.
- Carry-forward sites (`routes/priorities.py`, `routes/lifestyle.py`, `routes/cyberware.py`,
  `routes/character.py`'s save building) use `resources_gear_args` to preserve ratings across a
  priority reconfirm / shared-budget re-validation / save.
- `routes/character.py`: `_resources_selection_for_display` now takes the committed `gear` items
  directly (each already carries its final cost/rating) instead of reconstructing from names via the
  catalog — which was the pre-WP66 shape and would mis-cost every rating item at 0. The sheet's
  `gear_rows` are keyed by `(name, rating)` and the template shows `Name (Rating N) × qty`.

## Key decisions / traps

- **Foci/Formulae excluded** (documented in decisions-log 2026-07-29). A deliberate deviation from the
  WP's literal "load the rest of the catalog": those 64 rating items are already modeled by the Foci
  bonding engine (WP47) with Force/Karma semantics; offering them as plain gear would duplicate and
  misrepresent them. The flat-cost `Spell Formula, X` entries (also `Formulae` category) are a different
  thing and stay — the exclusion is scoped to rating-priced items.
- **The `Camera` collision** is real: `Camera` is both a 0-cost `Sensor Functions` component and the
  real `Rating * 100` `Vision Devices` item. Same class as WP21's documented `Vision Magnification`
  collision, but the flat side is 0-cost, so the plain "keep higher cost" tie-break would keep the
  wrong (0-cost) one — hence the dedicated "rating item displaces a 0-cost flat namesake" rule.
- **`CharacterSave` field ordering.** `rating_gear_picks` had to go in the *defaulted* block (after
  `quality_text`), not next to `gear_names` — `gear_names` is followed by the non-default
  `lifestyle_tier_name`/`lifestyle_months`, and a defaulted field can't precede a non-default one.
- **No rulebook lookup needed.** The Availability ≤ 12 rule and the cap approach were already settled
  (Project Overview 2026-07-21; the per-item-avail-parse cap by the 2026-07-27 WP66 ruling), so no PDF
  consult and no new `learnings.md` entry.
