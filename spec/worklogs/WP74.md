# WP74 — Priority-built magician + cyberware fixture (Essence→Magic loss), with foci/mentor

Phase S. Third fixture-authoring WP, the first to drive the magic subsystem through WP72's synthetic
harness. Closes coverage gaps **G3** (a magic-enabled character whose cyberware Essence loss reduces
the Magic rating — the single cross-phase link between Phase I's Magic rating and Phase J's Essence
tracking) and **G6** (Foci + Mentor Spirit on a magician build) — see `phase-s-test-fixtures.md` §2.
The corpus's only two magic Priority builds (Miko, Wesson) carry zero cyberware, so the Essence→Magic
interaction had never had a whole-character fixture, and nothing combined a magician with a bonded
focus *and* a mentor spirit.

## Goal

Extend `SyntheticCharacter`/`build_synthetic()` with magic inputs (the WP72-documented "add your
field + wiring together" pattern), then author one legal, fully-spent magician Priority build carrying
enough cyberware to drop Essence below a whole point — proving the reduced Magic rating flows into the
downstream caps and MAG-linked dice pools, plus the focus/mentor-granted Improvements, every asserted
number cited to `priorities.xml`/`cyberware.xml`/`foci.xml`/`mentors.xml`/`traditions.xml` or an
already-extracted `learnings.md` formula.

## Log

### Harness extension (tests-only, no `src/` change)

Like WP72/73, tests-only. The big structural change: a magic build spends a full Priority slot on
**Talent**, so the harness could no longer force Talent to E (mundane) and take only four letters.

- `SyntheticCharacter` gains `talent_letter` (the fifth Priority slot) plus `magic_type`/`tradition`/
  `magic_granted_skills`/`spells`/`mentor_spirit`/`foci`, all name-keyed like the existing fields.
  When `magic_type` is set, `talent_letter` is required; `build_synthetic()` now validates all
  **five** category letters as a permutation of A-E (a mundane fixture still leaves `talent_letter`
  unset → Talent defaults to E, and the other four remain A-D, so WP72/73 are unchanged).
- `build_synthetic()` resolves the magic selection right after `create_character` (before qualities,
  since a Mentor Spirit pick needs `magic_type`), drives `create_magic_selection` +
  `apply_magic_skill_grant_improvements`, then threads Mentor Spirit through
  `create_quality_selection` + `apply_mentor_spirit_improvements`. **Spells and foci are resolved
  *after* cyberware/bioware on purpose** — both read `character.attribute_value("MAG")` for their
  caps (spell "Magic Rating x 2", foci count ≤ Magic / Force ≤ Magic x 2), so they must see the
  Essence-reduced rating, which is exactly the G3 interaction the fixture exists to prove.
- New metric keys: `magic:rating`, `magic:rating_before_essence_loss`, `magic:essence_loss`,
  `drain_resistance`, `power_points`, `foci:nuyen_cost`, `foci:karma_cost`, `spells:count`,
  `spells:karma_cost`.

Deliberately **not** added: `magic_attribute_points` (raising MAG with Special Attribute Points). No
current fixture needs it — the point here is Essence *loss*, not growth — so per the "don't build past
what's verified" discipline it stays out until a fixture exercises it. Only single-General-option
mentor archetypes are supported (the General slot auto-resolves); a two-option archetype (Cat, Fire-
Bringer, Wise Warrior) would need a choice field added — flagged in the dataclass docstring.

### The fixture — Sabine "Cinder" Aumann, Human Hermetic combat mage

Priorities **Talent A / Attributes B / Skills C / Resources D / Heritage E**. Every figure
hand-derived, then re-verified by building through the engine before writing the literal:

- Attributes (20 primary, all spent; EDG from Heritage E/Human's 1 special pt): BOD 4, AGI 2, REA 4,
  STR 1, CHA 2, INT 4, LOG 6 (the one natural max), WIL 5, EDG 3.
- Magic: Talent A grants Magic **6**, a 10-spell budget, and 2 free Magical-Active skills at rating 5
  (Spellcasting, Counterspelling). Hermetic tradition (Drain = LOG + WIL).
- Cyberware (Essence **0.3**, 4,000¥): Datajack 0.1, Image Link 0.1, Vision Magnification 0.1.
  Essence 6 − 0.3 = **5.7**; `ceil(0.3) = 1` Magic lost → finished Magic **5**.
- Skills (Skills C = 28 + 2 group, all spent): Conjuring group 2 (Summoning/Banishing/Binding);
  individual Assensing 4, Astral Combat 3, Arcana 3, Perception 4, Survival 2, Pistols 3, Sneaking 2,
  Con 2, Negotiation 2, First Aid 3. Counterspelling ends **6** (grant 5 + Mountain Magician +2 = 7,
  capped at 6); Survival ends **4** (2 bought + Mountain General +2).
- Spells: 7 of the free 10 (Manabolt, Stunbolt, Powerbolt, Fireball, Increase Reflexes, Improved
  Invisibility, Heal); 7 ≤ Magic 5 × 2 = 10, so 0 leftover Karma.
- Foci: one Power Focus at Force 2 (36,000¥, 12 Karma; Avail 8 ≤ 12; count 1 ≤ 5, Force 2 ≤ 10). Its
  own dice bonus is `NOT_YET_SUPPORTED` (unmodeled — the augmentation posture), so only its cost is
  asserted.
- Qualities: Mentor Spirit (Mountain) 5 + Indomitable (Mental) 8 − SINner (National) 5 = net 8 Karma.
- Derived: Drain Resistance 11 (LOG 6 + WIL 5), power_points 0 (Magician ≠ Adept), initiative 8,
  physical limit 4, mental limit 8 (7 + Indomitable), social limit 5 (ESS 5.7 → 6), physical CM 10,
  stun CM 11.
- Karma: 25 − 8 (qualities) − 12 (foci) = **5** left.

## Design decisions

- **Sub-1.0 Essence loss is the deliberate headline.** 0.3 Essence lost still costs a *full* Magic
  point — the cleanest possible test of p.278's "for every point OR FRACTION THEREOF" rule, which a
  larger, whole-number loss wouldn't isolate. Asserted on both sides
  (`magic:rating_before_essence_loss` 6, `magic:essence_loss` 1, `magic:rating` 5) plus its
  downstream flow into `pool:Spellcasting` (rating 5 + reduced MAG 5) and the spell/foci caps.
- **Mentor = Mountain, for two modeled slots.** Mountain is the one offered archetype whose General
  *and* Magician slots are both `FLAT_BONUS` (+2 Survival, +2 Counterspelling), so a single mentor
  pick exercises both improvement paths. Counterspelling deliberately lands the grant-5 + mentor-2 =
  7 → cap-6 interaction (the same skill-cap-on-improvements case WP72's Gymnastics proved, now on a
  Talent-granted skill).
- **Power Focus over a FLAT_BONUS focus.** The iconic combat-mage focus, and its selection is the
  richer test: its count/Force caps read the *reduced* Magic, so the Essence→Magic loss constrains
  the foci selection itself, not just skills. Its own bonus is unmodeled (like Smartlink/Bone Lacing
  in WP73) — cost asserted, effect not.
- **Spells/foci after cyberware in `build_synthetic`.** Ordering is load-bearing, not cosmetic — both
  caps key off the finished, Essence-reduced MAG; resolving them before cyberware would silently use
  Magic 6 and mis-size the caps.

## Traps hit

- **`skill_rating` caps improvements at 6.** Counterspelling's grant + mentor sums to 7 but resolves
  to 6 — caught before writing the literal by building through the engine first (the prototype
  printed rating 6, not 7). Asserted the capped 6, matching legacy `MaxSkillRatingCreate`.
- **Heritage E offers only Human.** The one metatype at Heritage E — fine for a Hermetic mage, and
  its single special point still lifts Edge to 3. Chose the priority spread around that constraint
  (Talent A being non-negotiable for a strong mage) rather than fighting it.
- **`ruff` line-length (100)** on the prose comment block and long citation strings — reflowed by
  hand (`ruff format` doesn't rewrap comments/strings), the same trap WP72/73 recorded. Also: an em
  dash (`→`, 1 char) vs. ASCII `->` (2 chars) subtly changes line length — noted while trimming the
  name string. No logic impact.
- No PDF lookup needed: every formula and budget was already extracted in `learnings/phase-d.md`
  (derived stats), `phase-i.md` (Essence→Magic loss p.278, Drain, Power Points), and `phase-m.md`
  (foci Force cap p.98/317, Mentor Spirit p.320-324), or lives in the catalogs/`priorities.xml`. So
  no new `learnings.md` entry and no `decisions-log.md` entry were warranted.
