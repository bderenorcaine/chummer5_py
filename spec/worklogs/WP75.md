# WP75 — Step-Seven leftover-Karma spend fixture

Phase S. Fourth fixture-authoring WP, the first to drive Phase O's Step Seven (leftover-Karma) spend
through WP72's synthetic harness. Closes coverage gaps **G4** (Karma-funded Active skill/skill-group
increases, *including* the group-breaking case, plus a Karma-funded specialization) and **G5** (extra
spells beyond the free Talent budget) — see `phase-s-test-fixtures.md` §2. Both mechanics were
explicitly recorded as fixture-less: `learnings/phase-o.md`'s WP53 entry ("No fixture in the 34-file
corpus exercises Karma-funded skill/group/specialization spend at all") and its WP54 entry ("No
fixture ... exercises Karma-funded extra spells" — Miko's 6 spells fit inside her free budget of 10,
Wesson is an Adept with no spell budget at all). This WP is the first executable coverage of either.

## Goal

Extend `SyntheticCharacter`/`build_synthetic()` with Step Seven's Karma-spend inputs (the
WP72-documented "add your field + wiring together" pattern), then author one legal, **fully-spent**
Priority build whose leftover Karma buys: an attribute point, an individual skill raise that breaks a
priority-bought skill group, a Karma-funded specialization, a skill group raised *as a group*, and a
spell beyond the free Talent budget — every asserted number cited to `priorities.xml`/the Karma
Advancement Tables already extracted in `learnings/phase-o.md`.

## Log

### Harness extension (tests-only, no `src/` change)

Like WP72-74, tests-only.

- `SyntheticCharacter` gains `karma_attribute_points`, `karma_skill_points`, `karma_group_points`,
  `karma_specializations` — each mirroring its priority-budget-funded sibling field's shape
  (`attribute_points`/`skill_points`/`group_points`/`specializations`) and meaning *additional*
  points on top of what the priority budget already funded. The three skill-side maps are name-keyed
  like their siblings; `karma_attribute_points` is keyed by attribute code.
- `build_synthetic()` resolves them **after** cyberware/bioware but **before** the spell block, in
  the order attributes → skills, each with `karma_budget=character.karma_remaining`. That order is
  not arbitrary: it's exactly what `chummer5.io.save_format.build_character` documents for the same
  three spends (attributes → skills → knowledge → spells), so every later spend's budget already
  reflects the earlier ones' cost and no two can double-spend the same Karma. Following it also means
  the harness and the save/load path can't disagree about what a given input literal costs.
- The Karma-funded-**spell** side needed no new field: a fixture simply declares more `spells` than
  its Talent option's free budget, and `create_spell_selection` (already wired by WP74) splits free
  vs. Karma-funded itself. Only the budget it's validated against moved (it now sees what the
  attribute/skill spends left).
- New metric keys: `karma_attribute:karma_cost`, `karma_skill:karma_cost`,
  `karma_skill:broken_groups` (a `frozenset[str]`). The Karma-funded attribute's resulting *value*
  needed no new key — `attr:<CODE>` already sums both point sources.

Deliberately **not** added: `karma_knowledge_points` (WP53's Knowledge/Language sibling selection).
No Phase-S fixture declares a Knowledge/Language roster at all yet, so per the harness's own
"don't build past what a fixture exercises" discipline it stays out — flagged as a remaining gap
below rather than speculatively wired.

### The fixture — Yseult "Wren" Marchetti, Elf Shamanic face

Priorities **Attributes A / Skills B / Talent C / Heritage D / Resources E**. Every figure
hand-derived first, then re-verified by building through the engine in a scratch prototype before the
literal was written (the WP74 practice — the prototype printed every rating/pool/limit, and all 60-odd
asserted values matched the hand derivation on the first run):

- Attributes (24 primary, all spent; **0** special points): BOD 3, AGI 4, REA 4, STR 3, CHA 8 (her one
  attribute at its natural maximum), INT 5, LOG 3, WIL 5. EDG starts at the Elf floor of **1**.
- Magic: Talent C's Magician option — Magic **3**, a 5-spell free budget, and **no free-skill grant**.
  Shamanic tradition (Drain = WIL + CHA = 13, her best pool of any kind). No cyberware, so no
  Essence→Magic loss; known-spells cap = 3 × 2 = **6**.
- Skills (Skills B = 36 + 5 group, all spent): groups Influence 4 + Conjuring 1; individual
  Spellcasting 5, Counterspelling 2, Assensing 3, Con 5 (+ a *skill-point-funded* "Fast Talk"
  specialization), Impersonation 3, Performance 2, Intimidation 3, Perception 3, Sneaking 2, Pistols 2,
  First Aid 2, Arcana 2, Disguise 1 (35 + 1 spec = 36).
- Qualities: Indomitable (Social) 8 positive; Codeblock 10 + SINner (Corporate Limited) 15 negative =
  **25, the Negative cap exactly**. Net quality Karma −17, so the Step Seven pool is 25 + 17 = **42**.
- Step Seven, all 42 spent (`karma_spent` 25, `karma_remaining` **0**):
  - EDG 1 → 2: **10** (new rating 2 × 5)
  - Negotiation 4 → 5: **10** (new rating 5 × 2) — **breaks the Influence group**: Negotiation is
    individually tracked at 5, Etiquette/Leadership stay at 4.
  - Negotiation specialization "Bargaining": **7** flat — the second, independent group-breaking
    trigger, landing on the same skill as the raise.
  - Conjuring group 1 → 2: **10** (new rating 2 × 5) — raised *as a group*, legal precisely because
    nothing broke that group.
  - A 6th spell: **5** — one beyond the free 5, landing exactly on the Magic × 2 cap.
- Derived: initiative 9, physical limit 5, mental limit 6, social limit **10** (9 + Indomitable
  (Social)), physical CM 10, stun CM 11, Essence 6, Drain Resistance 13, power_points 0.

## Design decisions

- **Elf at Heritage D, for two boundary cases the fixture set didn't have.** Heritage D/Elf funds
  **0** special attribute points (the existing fixtures have 5/4/1), so Edge sits at the metatype
  floor and *only* Step Seven Karma can lift it — which is exactly the mechanic this WP has to test,
  so the priority spread and the Karma spend reinforce each other rather than being independent
  choices. Elf is also the first non-Human/non-Ork metatype in the synthetic set, and its CHA 3-8
  range is what makes a CHA-8 Face possible at all.
- **Talent C, not A — a secondary caster by design.** Talent C's Magician row grants Magic 3, a
  5-spell budget, and **`skill_qty` unset** — the first fixture to exercise a Talent option with no
  free-skill grant (WP74's Talent A grants two skills at 5). It also makes G5 tight rather than
  theoretical: with Magic 3 the known-spells cap is 6, so the single Karma-funded spell sits exactly
  *on* the cap, where an off-by-one in `create_spell_selection`'s "combined free + Karma" reading
  (the trap `learnings/phase-o.md`'s WP54 entry warns about) would show up immediately.
- **Both group-breaking triggers, on the same skill.** WP53's research found breaking a group has two
  independent triggers — an individual Karma raise *and* a Karma specialization. Negotiation takes
  both, so a regression in either path fails this fixture; the untouched siblings (Etiquette,
  Leadership) are asserted at the unchanged group rating 4, which is the actual content of the
  "broken group" rule rather than just the `broken_groups` set membership.
- **A second group raised as a group, to cover the group cost formula.** `KARMA_SKILL_GROUP_PER_RATING`
  (5/rating) had no fixture coverage. Conjuring 1 → 2 covers it, and the same submission's
  Influence break proves the engine's "a group can't be both raised and broken in one submission"
  rule isn't over-applied (the conflict check is per-group, not global).
- **The attribute spend (WP52) is included, though the WP's own bullet lists only skills/spec/spell.**
  The WP entry names "the WP52/WP53 cost formulas" and depends on WP52 explicitly, and
  `karma_attribute_selection` was an entirely fixture-uncovered `Character` field feeding both
  `attribute_value` and `karma_spent`. One EDG point covers it for 10 Karma and doubles as the
  character's only route off the Heritage-D Edge floor.
- **Fully spent to zero.** The quality ledger was chosen (positives 8, negatives at the 25-Karma cap)
  so that the five Step Seven spends sum to exactly the 42-Karma pool, i.e. `karma_spent` 25 /
  `karma_remaining` 0. That's the tightest possible check on the chained-budget wiring: any spend
  charging one Karma too much would push a later `create_*` call over its budget and raise.
- **Team fit (roster §5).** Shamanic (CHA) vs. WP74's Hermetic (LOG); social specialist vs. artillery;
  fills Cheshire's deliberate CHA-2 gap. Kept away from the Decker's lane on purpose — she has
  Codeblock and LOG 3, so WP76's Matrix specialist still has a job.

## Traps hit

- **Evaluation-order choice was load-bearing and non-obvious.** The harness already resolved spells
  before foci (WP74, for the Essence→MAG cap). `learnings/phase-o.md`'s WP54 entry records that
  `save_format.build_character` had to *move* its spell resolution to after every other Step Seven
  spend for exactly this reason; the harness now mirrors that order rather than inventing its own, so
  a fixture's Karma arithmetic means the same thing through both paths.
- **`create_karma_skill_selection` measures a broken member's raise from the group's rating, not from
  the skill's own (zero) points.** Negotiation's raise costs `(4+1) × 2 = 10`, not `1 × 2 = 2` —
  confirmed in `skills.py`'s broken-group branch before pricing the build. Getting this wrong would
  have made the "fully spent to zero" ledger silently wrong by 8 Karma.
- **`apply_quality_improvements` takes `selection.qualities`, not the selection.** Hit in the scratch
  prototype (the harness itself already does this correctly) — noting it because the next prototype
  will hit it again.
- **`ruff` line-length (100)** on the prose block and long citation strings, the same trap every prior
  Phase-S WP recorded (`ruff format` won't rewrap comments/strings). Also note `tests/` has
  pre-existing `ruff format` drift in 12 unrelated files — left alone; the three files this WP touched
  are clean.
- No PDF lookup needed: all five cost formulas (attribute `new×5`, skill `new×2`, group `new×5`, flat
  7 per specialization, 5 per spell), the group-breaking rule, the combined `Magic × 2` spell cap, and
  Edge's exemption from the natural-maximum rule (p.99) were already extracted in
  `learnings/phase-o.md` (WP51-54). So no new `learnings.md` entry and no `decisions-log.md` entry.
