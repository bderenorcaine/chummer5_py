# WP88 — The fixture: Jacob "Tithe" Smith, the wetwork specialist

Phase S. Closes coverage gaps **G8-G13** on top of WP85's data, WP86's purchase trees and WP87's
spend-side fields. Lands the team's sixth runner — and the first one this project didn't design.

## Goal

Author `WETWORK_FIXTURE` from `tests/fixtures/saves/razorboy.json` with the full hand-derived
`expected`/`citations` treatment WP72-76 established, and add the one metric key still missing for
G11.

## Log

### The one new harness capability: a conditional limit

`resolve_metric` had no way to ask for a limit *for a specific test*, so WP39/WP40's conditional
Improvements were unobservable on a finished character. Three of them sit on this build (Vision
Enhancement 3, Audio Enhancement 3, Spatial Recognizer), and without a key they would have been
present-but-unasserted — precisely the "hand-verified, no fixture" failure mode the phase exists to
end.

Grammar: `limit:mental@PERCEPTION_VISUAL`, where the suffix is a `ResolutionContext` member name.
`@` rather than a fourth `:` segment, because the existing `cyberware:<path>:<field>` key already
owns three-segment colon syntax and a path may itself contain arbitrary catalog names. The
context-free `limit:mental` keeps meaning the plain, unconditional figure a character sheet shows.

What makes the assertion say something is the *contrast*, so all four are in `expected`: the
unconditional Mental limit is **4**, visual and hearing Perception each read **7**, and finding a
sound's source reads **6** — Spatial Recognizer's +2, *not* cumulative with Audio Enhancement's +3,
because the conditions don't subsume one another (`engine/improvements.py`'s "no subsumption" rule).
A test additionally asserts `limit:mental@GYMNASTICS_JUMPING == 4`, i.e. a context with no bonus of
its own falls back rather than picking up a neighbour's.

### The Step Seven spend — and why the figure is 15, not 19

§6 recorded the player's file as leaving **19** Karma unspent and told WP88 to spend it. WP89 landed
in between and made Step Six's 4 converted Karma a real debit, so the leftover is **15**. Following
the §6 number uncritically would have produced a fixture that didn't build — which is what that
entry's "do not carry the Karma-to-Nuyen figure over uncritically" note was for.

15 spends exactly, and each half of it says something no existing fixture says:

- **A Karma specialization on Sneaking, "Urban" — 7 Karma flat.** Sneaking is a Stealth member and
  Stealth *was* group-purchased, so this breaks the group. WP75 already covers group-breaking and
  WP76 covers a specialization that breaks nothing; what's new is that it happens on a skill already
  at rating **7**, above `MAX_SKILL_RATING_CREATE`. Group-breaking and the over-cap rating had never
  met on one skill before.
- **Combat Tactics 3 → 4 and Area Knowledge (Seattle) 3 → 4 — 4 Karma each.** The first fixture to
  spend Karma on Knowledge skills at all. Phase S §4's own closing note named this as a deliberate
  hole for want of any fixture with a Knowledge roster; WP87 removed the cause and this removes the
  hole.

`karma_spent` = qualities 3 + contacts 3 + conversion 4 + specialization 7 + knowledge 8 = **25**,
`karma_remaining` 0. Every budget on this character is now spent to the pin: 20/20 attribute points,
1/1 special, 46/46 skill points, 10/10 group points, 12/12 Knowledge points, ¥282,420 of ¥283,000,
and 25/25 Karma. That is the §5 rationale working as intended — a fully-spent build hits the
validators at their exact limits.

This is the **only** deliberate divergence from the save file, and it's recorded in the fixture's own
header comment as well as here, per the WP entry's instruction that the save is the input of record.

### Derivation practice

Same as WP74-76: every figure hand-derived first, then a scratch prototype run against the engine
before the literal was written. All ~70 asserted values matched the hand derivation on the first run.
The two worth spelling out because they're the point of the fixture:

- **Essence 0.10** = 6 − (Bone Lacing 1.5 + Wired Reflexes r2 `FixedValues(2,3,5)[2]` 3 + Hand Blade
  0.25 + Shock Hand 0.25 + Cyberears `(3*0.1)+0.1` 0.4 + Cybereyes 0.4 + Datajack 0.1). Plugins cost
  Capacity, not Essence — which is why eight plugins add nothing to that sum.
- **Social limit 5**, not 4: `ceil((CHA 3*2 + WIL 3 + ceil(ESS 0.10))/3) = ceil(10/3) = 4`, +1
  Indomitable (Social). `ceil(0.10) = 1` is doing real work — a flat 0 gives 3+1 = 4. That is what
  §6 meant by the Essence floor being load-bearing rather than merely dramatic.

### The audit test, and why it matters more than the citations

`test_wetwork_fixture_agrees_with_the_save_file_it_was_transcribed_from` builds both the fixture and
`razorboy.json` and compares 36 metrics. Citations make each number *traceable*; this test makes the
whole transcription *auditable* — it would catch a mistyped attribute point or a dropped plugin,
which a citation cannot, because a citation only says where a number came from, not that the input it
was computed from is the one on disk. The excluded metrics are exactly the Step Seven divergence
above, asserted separately.

### Naming

Settled in the planning entry rather than here, and worth restating because the file makes it look
backwards: his SINner (National) quality names him **Jacob Smith**, which is his real,
government-registered identity. He is a SINner, not a nobody; the Rating-4 Fake SIN is the alias.
Handle **"Tithe"** — what he has left, one tenth (0.10 Essence).

### Roster check, run after the fact

Every WP73-76 fixture was designed *against* §5's spread table. Tithe wasn't — he was designed
against nothing, by a player. The check was therefore run afterwards, and it passes: he's the set's
only LOG-2 melee specialist and its only Essence-floor character, he duplicates nobody's attribute
peak (STR 5 where Rebar's is BOD/STR 6 with far more soak and no stealth at all), his AGI 4 sits
below Cheshire's, and his stealth lane is the rooftop approach rather than her locks-and-legwork B&E.
Recorded in §5 as a small piece of evidence that the design-against-each-other discipline was
producing a genuinely spread set, not one that merely looked spread from inside its own table.
