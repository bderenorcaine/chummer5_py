# WP89 — Karma-to-Nuyen conversion debits the unified Karma ledger

Phase O, reopened. Fixes a real rules bug found by importing a player-authored save: converting Karma
to nuyen at Step Six cost nothing. A character could take the full 10 Karma for ¥20,000 and still
spend all 25 Karma on qualities, contacts and Step Seven.

## Goal

Add `resources_selection.karma_converted` to `Character.karma_spent`, having first confirmed the
direction against the rulebook rather than assuming it, and rule on where the double-spend check
lives.

## Log

### This was a stale deferral, not an oversight — and that's the reusable part

`spec/worklogs/WP43.md` (2026-07-21) states the reasoning in as many words: Phase O hadn't landed, so
there was no running Karma total for the conversion to draw down, and wiring a check would have meant
inventing a ledger to check against. That was *true when written*. WP51 built the ledger three weeks
later and nothing existed to notice that a stated trigger condition had passed. `engine/resources.py`
was still carrying the gap in its module docstring as deliberate, which is exactly what it was — just
no longer.

The ruling recorded at planning time (`decisions-log.md`, 2026-08-01): **a deferral whose stated
trigger is another WP landing should name that WP in the *depending* phase file, not only in the
deferring WP's own worklog**, so the landing WP's author sees it. Filed forward rather than fixed
retroactively.

### Direction, confirmed three ways

The WP entry asked for a citation rather than an assumption, which was the right instinct — "the
Karma is spent" is intuitive but the opposite reading ("the conversion is a Resources-priority
perk") is not absurd on its face. Three independent confirmations, recorded in
`spec/rulebooks/learnings/phase-o.md`:

1. **p. 94 itself** (PDF page 96, via `pdftotext -layout`): "players can convert some of their Karma
   to nuyen if they need a little more to spend. They may convert up to 10 Karma at a rate of 2,000
   nuyen per Karma point for gear." *Convert*, and *some of their Karma* — a transformation of one
   pool, not an addition beside it.
2. **Step Seven's own title**, "Spending Your Left Over Karma" (p. 98) — left over from the steps
   before it, of which Step Six is one. A free conversion would make "left over" meaningless at
   precisely the word the rulebook chose.
3. **The legacy engine.** `Character.CalculateKarmaValue` computes the converted amount's Karma
   value and does `intReturn += intNuyenKarma` under the `Checkbox_CreatePACKSKit_StartingNuyen`
   line item, alongside qualities, contacts and skill points. Legacy counts it as spend.

Plus a corpus cross-check: `Wesson.chum5` has `<nuyenbp>1</nuyenbp>` and `<karma>2</karma>` remaining
against `<buildkarma>25</buildkarma>` — legacy's own remaining figure is net of the conversion.

### The ruling: the ledger, not a `karma_budget` parameter

The WP entry left this genuinely open ("either is defensible, but say which and why"). Every other
Karma-spending `create_*` function takes a `karma_budget` and validates against it, so the symmetric
answer would be to give `create_resources_selection` one too. It doesn't fit **this** spender, for a
reason specific to it:

`create_resources_selection` is **re-called several times within a single build** — once up front,
again once cyberware/bioware are resolved, again once foci are (`build_character`) — and Step Seven's
spends are resolved *in between two of those calls*. So one unchanged player choice would have to be
validated against three different Karma budgets, which is exactly the double-counting trap WP51's
"add this section's own committed cost back in" idiom exists to avoid. Making the conversion a *term
of* `karma_spent` instead is checked once, in one place, and needed no new parameter at any call
site — including the web route, which was untouched.

**The consequence, stated rather than glossed:** an over-conversion isn't rejected at its own commit
point. It drives `karma_remaining` negative and surfaces as every subsequent Step Seven spend being
refused. That is the same "validity is re-derived rather than enforced at construction" shape the
Project Overview's "Character as the single aggregate" section already describes for stale priority
selections, so it's consistent with the architecture rather than a hole in it — but it *is* a
trade, and the next person to look at a negative `karma_remaining` deserves to find it written down.
`karma_to_nuyen` still enforces p. 94's flat 0-10 cap, since that one is a property of the conversion
alone and needs no ledger.

### Blast radius: one expected value, and it moved toward legacy

Checked before assuming: the five synthetic fixtures all convert 0, and only the legacy-import path
(`io/legacy_import.py` maps `<nuyenbp>` onto this field) reaches a nonzero value in the test suite.
Exactly one test moved — `test_wesson_imports_end_to_end`'s `karma_remaining`, 10 → 9. That is the
intended direction and legacy agrees with it, so the assertion was updated with the reasoning inline
rather than treated as a regression.

`razorboy.json` now reports `karma_remaining: 15` rather than 19, which is what WP88's Step Seven
spend had to be re-derived against — the WP88 entry's own "do not carry the figure over uncritically"
note.
